# Next-stage ancestry confirmation design v1

This is a reviewable proposal, not a runner configuration or an executed experiment. No final ancestry cohort was generated or scored by this task. Root owns subsequent integration, the final freeze and resource scheduling. The design tests a possible useful ancestry benefit and presumes neither acceptance, novelty nor superiority.

## What the artifacts establish

The ancient/inferred Q64 configurations define 16 TRAIN and four VAL independent parents, TEST 0, a 1 Mb chromosome, 32 query diploids, three reference diploids per source, 128 fixed midpoint coordinates and eight tiles. Relate 1.2.4 uses cleaned observed genotypes, observed sample dates, a uniform synthetic map and simulated ancestral polarization at observed sites. All 2,560 source-parent grid points were retained. The inferred manifest reports minimum physical coverage 0.998559, native inference total 21.05 s and total preparation 333.28 s over 20 parents.

The completed inferred boundary result has seed 101 only. Its best boundary comparator is GRU at VAL NLL 1.0382433, compared with boundary_full 1.0386516. Available new-anchor files include plain depth 1 1.0308086, plain depth 2 1.0256467 and softplus depth 2 1.0264802. The selected plain depth 2 versus GRU paired parent NLL differences, candidate minus baseline, are [-0.0288700, -0.0012456, +0.0033154, -0.0235863]. Mean is -0.0125966 and sample SD 0.0159962. These are four reused validation parents, one seed and selected checkpoints. They are development evidence, not independent confirmation or accurate population variance estimates. Historical metrics cap probabilities at 1e-12.

The meta-review's independent-confirmation concern remains valid after these encouraging selection scores. The full plain/age/control studies need not have completed merely because individual model JSON files exist. The ARGMix full-tree adaptation's current one-seed VAL NLL 1.1422965 does not excuse weak baseline tuning. Canonicalization has CPU contract/timing evidence and no ancestry training result. Source hashes and exact paths are retained in the accompanying JSON.

## Fixed independent confirmation and practical precision

After model, baseline, three checkpoints per model, analysis code, admission rules and secondary comparisons are frozen and hashed, generate a separate fixed cohort of160 admitted independent ID parents. Proposed masters are20261111 and20261112 under confirmatory-v1. Do not generate or inspect these inputs during development. Attempt IDs0..191 in fixed order and use the first 160 label-blind admitted parents. If192attempts cannot yield160, report the entire attempt ledger and analyze the admitted cohort once with the precision shortfall disclosed. Do not increase N after observing test differences or their variance.

For parent i and training seed s, compute L_is as the mean float64 stable log-softmax NLL over all query haplotypes and original128 midpoints retained in inferred SNP coverage. Define d_i as the average over the three individual-model loss differences, candidate minus selected baseline. The primary estimate is the equally weighted mean of d_i over parents. This evaluates three fixed trained models. It is not a probability ensemble and not an estimate based on480 independent parent-seed cells.

Set a pragmatic practical benefit threshold of0.010 nats per parent and target95% interval half-width 0.005 nats. A0.010 reduction is roughly1% of exploratory NLL near1.04 and is a modest useful improvement, not a universal biological criterion. Planning SD0.030 is deliberately larger than the selected four-parent SD0.016. No power claim follows from that pilot.

N160 therefore does not guarantee the target. Report achieved precision and do not use optional stopping.

The primary95% interval and two-sided test use paired-parent t inference, df=N−1, alpha 0.05. Report a10,000-resample paired-parent bootstrap percentile interval as a prespecified sensitivity. Resample the entire parent vector across seeds/methods/conditions, never individual tiles, positions, queries or parent-seed entries. Use fixed analysis seed 20261115. The interpretation is conditional on the frozen TRAIN cohort and the three selected checkpoints. Show all three seed-specific mean differences, their range/SD and parent vectors separately. Three seeds are too few for a reliable unconditional training-seed uncertainty claim.

An upper95% bound below0 supports a statistically favorable result, subject to sensitivity. An upper bound below-0.010 establishes an improvement beyond the practical threshold. A point estimate below-0.010 with an upper bound between-0.010 and0 is favorable but does not establish the practical magnitude. An interval including0 is inconclusive. If t and bootstrap sign-exclusion conclusions disagree, disclose the disagreement and avoid a robust-positive claim. A narrow null can be informative, but do not manufacture equivalence from a nonsignificant test.

## Limited secondary mechanism comparisons

Freeze at most three secondary paired-parent NLL contrasts, independently of test outcomes, with Holm family-wise alpha 0.05:

1. Selected full candidate versus same-geometry/depth gain-matched diagonal.
2. Selected full candidate versus same-schema/depth static local control.
3. If age is selected, age full versus matched scalar-age full. Otherwise, if plain depth 2 is selected, contextual depth 2 versus noncontextual depth 2 query-source feedback. No third contrast is needed for plain depth 1.

Give each eligible control the same three seeds and at most eight configurations selected on development VAL. These controls are described in docs/anchor_controls.md and frozen factorial configs. Static local retains graph-local mixing, gain-matched diagonal retains instantaneous source mixing, and noncontextual retains global kernel messages and decoder depth. They test specific information paths and are not graph-free nulls. If age is selected, feedback attribution stays developmental unless assigned a separate independent protocol. Complete all three propagation variants and age controls in the developmental record without converting the entire grid into a test family.

Report these contrasts, estimates and corrected results even if the primary result is unfavorable. Other final diagnostics are descriptive. A primary predictive advantage does not by itself establish which mechanism caused it.

## Admission, absence and failed runs

Apply identical label-blind admission before windowing: successful native inference, exact sampled-haplotype/coordinate correspondence, no truth-bearing observation fields, finite valid ages and strictly positive resistance, the existing condition cap1e12, graph expansion<=1,000,000, nonempty SNP coverage, physical coverage>=0.95 and at least120 of128 original midpoints retained. Check full-parent electrical and canonicalization admissibility. Never select on source proportions, tract difficulty, purity, model NLL or label balance. Keep mixed inferred intervals and both query haplotypes. No individual tile/query can be discarded after scoring.

Retain attempted IDs, rejection/absence reason, coverage, SNP attrition, inferred counts, numerical diagnostics and resources. The endpoint is conditional on this declared admissibility; report admission probability alongside it. A missing class is not a rejected parent. Per-class undefined denominators are NA with support0. Preserve all four class slots and separately disclose the existing fixed-zero all-class macroF1 convention. No true boundaries means undefined boundary recall/F1, not zero false-boundary cost.

Permit one same-seed/config/input retry for infrastructure failure and preserve its first receipt. A deterministic code defect found before freeze requires a documented fix and rerun of every affected family, not a selectively repaired winner. Unresolved numerical model failure makes that developmental configuration/seed ineligible and its failure rate visible. During final evaluation, do not switch to a runner-up or remove a parent because one method fails. An unresolved primary prediction failure prevents a positive primary confirmation; publish failure counts and clearly labeled complete-case descriptive sensitivity. A shared bug discovered after unblinding compromises v1. Preserve its results and do not label a corrected reuse of the same cohort independent confirmation.

## Complete metrics and actual held-out boundary

In addition to primary NLL, report accuracy, multiclass Brier, four-class macroF1, confusion/support, each class's precision/recall/F1, diploid dosage MAE,15 fixed equal-width calibration bins and classwise reliability. Report raw and VAL-temperature-calibrated calibration separately. Preserve the historical1e-12-floor NLL for compatibility while primary NLL comes directly from stable logit evaluation without hidden clipping.

Use 4096 fixed physical midpoint readouts for descriptive tract metrics. These are additional targets and must never add source anchors. Report approximate physical-span quadrature scores with that approximation stated. Use one-to-one boundary matching within1000 bp, switch-count absolute error per haplotype, class-specific tract-length errors, true short tracts<=10 kb and positions within10 kb of artificial tile boundaries versus interior. Dense grid spacing is approximately 244 bp and does not resolve arbitrarily short tracts. Keep all missed/extra boundaries and report denominators. Physical coordinates are bp, model x is bp/1e6, rates are per unit x and ages are generations. Any tile sensitivity uses these same units without resetting the rate scale.

Before lock, no confirmatory labels, genotypes, inferred graphs, geometry or predictions are generated or accessed. After lock, a custodian preparation process may use truth for target extraction and integrity assertions. Development/model-selection processes receive no test labels, input trees, class counts or predictions. Only hashes, attempted/admitted counts, label-free coverage, resource/numerical QA and reason codes may be released. This boundary is operational and must be implemented with separate paths/process permissions or equivalent sealed artifacts; writing it here does not enforce it.

The frozen evaluation runner then predicts from observed inputs with no gradients or optimization. Seal all method/seed predictions and the admission ledger before joining labels. Run the joined analysis/report once. No outcome-driven scale, checkpoint, seed, cohort, mask or threshold change follows. Output-only repairs may reuse identical prediction/analysis hashes and must be logged. The report contains all principal/secondary results, achieved precision, calibration, class/tract diagnostics, failures and coverage.
