The original label producer in `src/arggnn/benchmarking/european_sampling.py:183` writes `result[record['example_index']]`; therefore the loader's `labels[indices]` is the correct canonical join, independent of the registry's position-sorted storage. Registry duplicates and repeated random draws retain multiplicity.

The observation adapter uses an explicit whitelist. Independent tests reject an added labels field both before coordinate expansion and before model preparation. The separate labels vector is suitable for the loss. No ancestry target is used in graph construction, cohort frequencies, cuts or source weights. This is a source-and-contract finding, not a universal information-flow proof for arbitrary future callers.

Observed cohort is biologically consequential: the native target schema restricts BAA queries to classes 5/6 and other query cohorts to classes 1-4. Legitimate cohort metadata can therefore explain substantial recovery from the prior failed fit. That is intended information parity, not evidence for transport. The nine cohorts, known reference classes, dates and source genotype calls must stay identical across arms. Unknown query ancestry remains -1. Population/cohort identity must not be relabelled as locus ancestry, and same-leaf identity denotes the observed haplotype, not a stable internal ancestral individual.

Uniform global registry-row draws preserve the selected empirical target risk. Genomic source weights and target-loss weights are separate. A fixed sampled subset remains a diagnostic subset, not the complete 50,000-row TRAIN risk. On node-budget microbatches, the existing denominator contract preserves the original fixed draw count; separate optimizer steps per variable-sized subgroup would change weighting. TRAIN002 must remain completely excluded from fitting. Correlated SNPs/windows do not provide 64 independent biological replicates; the parent is the replication unit.

The mapped tree path is an exact restricted fast path, not generic tskit random access. Persistent structures unsupported by its proof fail closed. Large-source SHA256 values are inherited authenticated references; this bounded review did not independently rehash the 8.8-GB tree, full VCF or large inference JSON. ## Coordinate transport and mathematical guarantees

The final adapter validates a single forced focal coordinate per window with zero continuous source weight/density. Random coordinates use the declared uniform proposal and normalized quadrature mass. It expands every coordinate draw to a complete tree occurrence, including duplicate selections. This spends extra computation but preserves the measure and avoids silently collapsing nonlinear target updates. Every focal maps to its own forced token. Source weights are not multiplied by native interval length. Independent tests verify the numerical effect of duplicated draw mass, exclusion of an arbitrarily large zero-weight forced source value, and exactly zero other-window input gradient.

The primitive partition map preserves constants and leaf-weighted latent sums and contracts the associated squared norm. An independent unequal-clade fixture verifies those properties. They are conditional-expectation/pooling facts, not new optimal transport or evidence that latent conservation is biological conservation. Learned projections, temporal normalization, residuals and classification have no blanket mass or PSD guarantee.

An explicit witness in `algebra_counterexamples.json` shows normalized temporal mixing changing original physical mass from 1 to 0.9466958864. Another witness shows exact raw integral additivity while target subdivision followed by squaring gives 0.4128102972 rather than the coarse update's 0.4103332474. Source subdivision additivity must not be promoted to nonlinear target-refinement invariance.

Fixed coordinate draws protect routing from artificial tree-record subdivision only if the attributed observation field is unchanged. This model still pools SNP mean/max/logcount over native intervals. Splitting those intervals can change inputs, so full-predictor subdivision invariance is not established. Uniform source quadrature is a linear-integration construction; self-normalization is a ratio estimator, and nonlinear predictions/gradients are not unbiased dense-context estimates.

The retained complete tree may extend beyond the 50-kb sampling domain, and its full SNP pool is retained. Thus 50 kb bounds sampled coordinates rather than every observation influencing the prediction. K=5 can miss short informative contexts. The direct focal-SNP route allows within-tree variation but does not prove dense ancestry-boundary fidelity. Short-tract deletion, false switches and seam effects require separate dense evaluation before biological claims.
