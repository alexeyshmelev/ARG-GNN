## Scientific decisions

The new model may change its architecture. Simulations, author solutions and canonical labels are fixed. Cross-tree overlap is averaging over shared descendants and ages, not proof of ancestor identity. Sliding graph windows already have prior art. Test accuracy cannot select architecture. Internal development comes from original TRAIN chromosomes held out from the corresponding fit. Original benchmark training and internal development comparisons must have separate output paths and explicit fitting support.

Efficiency means completed useful training work and memory cost with a supplied accelerator. Do not inflate activity with redundant computation. Report sustained device activity along with examples per second, phase timing, CPU/GPU peaks and class-level learning. Short fixed-batch profiles do not qualify the entire pipeline. No arbitrary wall-time experiment cutoff.

The previous 128/64 pilot establishes learnability only and is not to be repeated for model selection. Qualification failures and unsuccessful designs remain in versioned reports. This plan does not resume the old full-parent TreeLoom queue or change native competitor settings.
