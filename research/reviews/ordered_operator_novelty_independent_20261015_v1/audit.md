**Independent ordered-tree operator audit — 15 October 2026**

**Assessment.** H046 is a defensible, specialized prediction hypothesis. Useful cross-tree gain and publication novelty remain unestablished. The exact support-excluded clade-route/readout combination was not found in the primary methods inspected, but this bounded comparison cannot establish priority. It does not justify a claim of a new temporal kernel or general graph-collection architecture. H046 is training, with no VAL accuracy available to this audit. H043's 0.0153 pp and H045's 0.009568 pp foreign-versus-own accuracy differences are below their 0.10 pp development criterion. H044's localization difference was 0.005260 pp with a boundary-F1 loss of about 3.138 pp. Those findings weaken, but do not refute, H046's new conditional question.

**Exact learned operator.** The inherited H042 body has 30,788 parameters: mutation/SNP-informed complete-tree propagation, a local gained/lost-clade flag adapter between two complete tree sweeps, leaf contexts, and a genomic BiGRU. Its flag adapter is O[gain*(Uh)*(Vh) - loss*(Uh)*(Wh)], using the current tree's state only. It therefore already supplies topology-change information without exchanging foreign states.

H046 takes first-sweep proper-clade states h_i before that flag injection. Exact stable sampled-descendant sets define maximal consecutive episodes. Roots and singletons are excluded, an absent clade breaks its episode, and equal unary clades use the deepest representative. For a query it selects the deepest containing proper clade whose episode has at least two native holds. This is descendant-set alignment, not inferred ancestral identity.

On original native hold J_i=[l_i,r_i), its projected field is f(y)=A h_j on every episode hold J_j, A:32→4. At scored x in J_i:

    v_L(x) = (1/tau) ∫_[episode_start,l_i) exp(-(x-y)/tau) f(y) dy
    v_R(x) = (1/tau) ∫_[r_i,episode_end) exp(-(y-x)/tau) f(y) dy.

The masses c_L,c_R replace f by 1. Integrals exclude the entire original focal hold and are not mass-normalized. Two segmented affine scans implement z_out=exp(-w/tau) z_in+(1-exp(-w/tau))f. A shared learned tau lies between 100 and 100,000 bp, initially 5,000 bp. Exponential attenuation can distinguish physical proximity within an episode. There is no learned dense node correspondence or attention matrix.

At each original 100-bp scoring center, categorical reference-only allele log-compatibilities are averaged separately within the focal hold's left/right 1-kb windows. Let d=ell_R-ell_L and g=||d||²/(1+||d||²), with g=0 when either side has no usable called SNP. The 27-vector contains base logits, mean local compatibility, d, v_L, v_R, |v_R-v_L|, the two masses and callability. A shared bias-free 27→8→4 tanh head averages original/swapped flank views, then adds g*tanh(Oz) to the base logits. O starts at zero. A, H, O and tau add 377 parameters, totaling 31,165. The whole backbone remains trainable. The matched own-state arm substitutes v_L=c_L*A*h_i and v_R=c_R*A*h_i, retaining the same masses, eligibility, evidence, parameters and training exposure.

**Verified prior art and the remaining distinction.** These are primary-source architectural comparisons, not claims of identical implementations or published-score reproduction.

- [Whitehouse, Ray and Schrider 2024, Fig. 1 and GCN architecture](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msae223) already combine marginal-tree GNNs with GRUs over nodes and trees. Tree learning plus genomic recurrence is established. Their sequence-level inference differs from this query-resolved ancestry readout.
- [ARGformer, §2.1](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag438) explicitly exploits shared ancestral identifiers across samples and genomic intervals through path tokenization, masked pretraining and contrastive learning. Reusing shared genealogical structure is established. H046's descendant-set alignment avoids persistent internal-node IDs and does not implement ARGformer's token mechanism.
- [ARGMix, Methods 4.1 and 4.3](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.05.714076) already predicts ancient local ancestry using inferred marginal trees, reference labels and relative-TMRCA attention. Its inspected 29-node query/reference/ancestor representation does not describe H046's clade-episode flank integral. It remains a preprint in this comparison.
- [AncestralPaths, Supplementary Note 3 pp. 30–32](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06705-1) already uses ordered ancestral reference-neighbour distributions and node ages for local path ancestry. The supplement calls its classifier a CNN, while the released source defines Flatten→Dense512→Dropout→Dense6. Neither discrepancy nor H046's use of whole trees establishes novel ancestry inference.
- [Graph Matching Networks, §3.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/li19d.html) already inject cross-graph latent messages into node computation. [GST, §3.1 Steps 2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05116v1) pools graph tokens, exchanges collection context and returns it to nodes. H046's explicit episode membership and physical support differ, but cross-graph latent exchange and pool/exchange/readout cannot be claimed as new. GST peer-reviewed publication was not verified.
- [S4, §2.1, §2.3 and §2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.00396v3) explicitly relates continuous state-space models, recurrent discretizations and convolutional filters. H046 uses an elementary exact scalar exponential hold integral, whereas S4 §2.3 uses bilinear discretization. This does not make the exponential recurrence or affine semigroup a new principle. The valid invariance is numerical integration-mesh refinement with field, original exclusion supports and episode membership fixed. Recomputing SNP pooling or adding BiGRU steps changes the field, so whole-model/native-hold subdivision invariance is absent.

The candidate distinction is therefore **exact descendant-set episode routing plus focal-support-excluded physical flank context used in a bounded, local-SNP-conditioned within-hold correction**. Verified: the implementation has this composition, and the inspected nearest methods do not describe this exact composition. Unverified: first publication, non-obviousness, useful inductive bias, generalization, robustness, and superiority to a simple matched local decoder.

**What an experiment would establish.** Complete the already frozen H046 pair before modifying the design. A gain over own-state supports foreign latent-state use conditional on this common gate, geometry and trainable body. It cannot attribute that gain specifically to exact-clade routing, contiguity, the exponential kernel or the gate. A separately frozen distance/clade-size/eligibility/mass-matched foreign-route reassignment is needed for the narrower clade-route claim. An advantage over an independently trained matched continuation is also needed before crediting the whole H046 package relative to H042. Do not credit extra optimization/readout capacity from historical checkpoint comparisons.

Report the registered span accuracy and boundary rule, paired parent effects, short-tract precision/recall, callability and hold-width strata, NLL/calibration, and wall time/memory. The eight reused VAL simulations with one seed are development evidence. A retained architecture needs independent parents and replicated optimizer seeds before a stable biological-benefit claim. No current score establishes better real ancient ancestry or demographic conclusions.

Root supplied an operational cost observation: H046 paired 32-parent FP32 passes took about 405–442 s after cache warmup, versus about 114 s for the identical-backbone H047 objective pair, roughly 3.6–3.9 times as long. These are different runs/hypotheses, not controlled profiling. They nevertheless make 377 added weights an inadequate efficiency argument. A tiny accuracy difference must be weighed against real training/inference cost.

**Biological restrictions.** Equal descendant sets do not imply the same ancestor, population state or constant ancestry. Tree inference errors can fragment episodes or preserve a misleading enclosing clade. Self-support exclusion is not statistical independence: neighbouring trees reuse samples and correlated SNPs. One global bp scale is not a recombination-map or cohort-specific tract model. Local compatibility from ancient proxies can be misleading under drift, reference misspecification, LD and missingness. The foreign field alone cannot locate an otherwise unobserved switch inside a hold.

There are also exact capability limits. No eligible episode or no called SNP on either local side gives zero correction. Each class correction has magnitude at most g, so a base winner whose margin over another class exceeds 2g cannot be overtaken by that class. Thus H046 deliberately restricts rescue of confident mistakes and sparse-local-evidence cases. Side symmetrization concerns the supplied flank views only. The inherited body has no chromosome-reversal theorem.

**One next architecture hypothesis, conditional on the completed results.** If another model is warranted, test a **four-state distance-aware chain decoder on the original 100-bp scoring grid with disjoint-bin SNP evidence and H042 tree logits as emissions**. Use every callable observed SNP once in its bin in this new evidence channel, missing evidence as zero, and a TRAIN-learned evidence scale to avoid pretending correlated SNP likelihoods are calibrated independent observations. Train the same tree/marker emission model with either chain marginals or independent per-bin softmax, using the unchanged original-grid exact-span training loss. Use one low-dimensional persistence law shared across positions, based on observed genetic distance when already available, otherwise explicitly physical distance. Do not append another clade adapter or demand a novel kernel.

This is a utility hypothesis: marker-supported within-hold switches plus explicit tract continuity can improve boundaries without creating H044's isolated corrections, while escaping H046's g-bound. It can fail because the proxy SNP evidence is weak, as H028/H031 already caution. It is not answered by H039, whose CRF runs only at native-hold boundaries, H033's tree/TV composition test, or H044's independent local residual head. [RFMix, Material and Methods / LAI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3738819/) already establishes reference-based emissions plus linear-chain CRF inference, so this should carry **no architecture-priority claim**. Its rationale is a documented resolution/tract bottleneck, not a decorative combination. A gain only in NLL, a boundary loss, or no paired advantage over the identical emission model would reject the proposed utility.
