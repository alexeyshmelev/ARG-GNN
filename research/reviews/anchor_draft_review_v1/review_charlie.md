### Summary

The study asks whether a persistent sampled interface, obtained by Kron reduction of changing marginal genealogies, can support useful nonlinear genomic context through graph-conditioned kernels. A graph-local encoder supplies bounded query and labeled-reference signals at fixed physical anchors. Positive-rate, age-conditioned query generators propagate these signals in the true interval order, with reference-inclusive row normalization and repeated anchor updates. The headline results presently are formal: a conditional PSD kernel certificate, bounded normalized messages, and invariance to two specified redundant subdivisions. The stated assumptions make these arguments credible, and the implementation audit provides meaningful supporting checks. There is no completed comparative result for the new anchor architecture in the frozen evidence, no final ancestry test, and no demonstrated predictive advantage of age geometry. That incompleteness is explicitly disclosed and appropriate for a preliminary draft, but the proposed full research article will need substantially stronger experimental attribution and independent evaluation.

### Major concerns

1. **Issue** — The proposed factorial can identify differences between model families without identifying whether useful transfer of previous query states, rather than source mixing or altered decay, causes those differences.
   **Where** — Abstract's statement that matched controls “separate” the mechanisms, Section 5.1, and Equations (10), (15), and (17).
   **Why it matters** — Scalar propagation changes the full generator diagonal as well as its off-diagonals, as the draft correctly acknowledges. Raw diagonal propagation changes gain, while gain-matched diagonal propagation applies an instantaneous cross-query source transform and matches only homogeneous continuous-forcing gain. Age geometry changes both the query generator and the reference source map. Even scalar propagation retains cross-query information through the plain local encoder. These are useful complementary controls, but no single difference uniquely attributes an improvement to the proposed memory mechanism. In particular, a gain from age branches could occur entirely through their reference forcing. The audit confirms these distinctions rather than eliminating them.
   **What would address it** — Predeclare which contrasts support which claims and require agreement across the existing controls. Add source-only versus propagation-only age interventions by separately fixing or changing the QR source block and QQ generator, with their matching ones-source normalization. Include a controlled task with known dependence on earlier query states and a changing-operator diagnostic demonstrating the finite-domain behavior of the competing controls. Treat an isolated full-versus-scalar improvement as a family comparison, not evidence that cross-query memory is the operative cause.

2. **Issue** — The final ancestry evaluation and statistical decision rules remain unspecified after substantial reuse of a very small validation cohort.
   **Where** — Section 5.2, `docs/anchor_pilot.md`, the frozen plain-anchor configuration, and `evidence.json` scope notes.
   **Why it matters** — Splitting parents before windowing and preserving the target boundary are sound design choices. However, the ancestry studies use 16 training and four validation parents, and the evidence describes one-seed exploratory comparisons and repeated validation reuse. The frozen anchor configuration alone compares nine models and selects checkpoints by validation NLL. Tiles, haplotypes, genomic coordinates, and checkpoint evaluations do not turn four independent validation parents into a well-powered confirmatory sample. A future improvement could reflect architecture selection on these parents. No independent ancestry test has been generated, so there is currently no generalization result to interpret.
   **What would address it** — Before confirmatory runs, freeze model selection, the age factorial, tuning budgets, a primary parent-level endpoint, the principal comparisons, and rules for any multiplicity adjustment. Generate independent ancestry test parents with sufficient replication chosen from a stated precision or power target. Report paired parent-level effect sizes and uncertainty, and repeat training seeds without treating seeds or correlated coordinates as substitute biological/simulation replicates. Keep these reused validation values explicitly exploratory. Reserve additional independently generated cohorts for robustness questions rather than repeatedly adapting to the same four parents.

3. **Issue** — The source feature bank and extra learned capacity provide strong alternative explanations for any eventual gain from kernel depth.
   **Where** — Section 2.2, Equations (13)–(20), and Section 5.1's admission that depth changes parameter counts.
   **Why it matters** — The 47 observed features already include source-conditioned common-ancestor times, reference fractions, nearest-reference votes, and fixed kernel evidence. The plain boundary encoder already mixes spatial evidence. The query residual path also retains the local embedding at every readout. Two kernel layers add projections, gating, and normalization as well as contextual exchange. Recording parameter counts is necessary but does not make a one-versus-two-layer contrast an exclusive depth/mechanism test. Earlier boundary, recurrent, and attention pilots cannot establish what the new anchor construction adds because they are distinct models, and the evidence explicitly says there is no established overall boundary-flow advantage.
   **What would address it** — Compare against strong feature-only predictors and simple sequence smoothing of the same source evidence, plus a same-anchor attention model and a local-capacity control with comparable active parameters and residual depth. Preserve the same observed information and scoring coordinates for attribution contrasts, while separately reporting appropriately implemented whole-tree ancestry baselines. Use fair validation tuning budgets rather than assuming equal hidden width or epoch count provides equal opportunity. Report source-feature ablations and query-only/reference-only message diagnostics so a gain cannot be attributed solely to already informative reference summaries.

4. **Issue** — The candidate's contribution remains a plausible composition of established ideas whose distinctive scientific value has not yet been demonstrated.
   **Where** — Section 1, Sections 3–4, and the absent completed anchor comparison.
   **Why it matters** — The draft appropriately avoids a first-method claim and credits established reduction and covariance theory. Related work nevertheless needs a sharper comparison: non-separable graph kernels derived from heat and wave SPDEs are already developed in [2111.08524v3], GraphSSM incorporates graph structure into a continuous-time state system through Laplacian regularization in [2406.00943v2], and MP-SSM integrates state-space principles into static and temporal message passing in [2505.18728v2]. Their inspected abstracts support this overlap, but do not settle whether they share this paper's precise contracts. The PSD covariance argument and serial-resistance telescoping appear correct under the stated assumptions, yet correctness of these inherited properties alone does not establish an AAAI-level contribution. The distinctive combination may be valuable, but that remains a hypothesis.
   **What would address it** — Provide a precise contribution map separating inherited mathematics, the fixed-interface/fixed-anchor design, and genuinely new guarantees or empirical capabilities. Compare the relevant operator, source, chronological-action, and subdivision assumptions with the closest methods using their actual formulations. Complete strong comparisons or clearly scoped adaptations. Show a task or regime in which the combination supplies a material accuracy, robustness, or computational benefit that simpler temporal kernels or graph state-space methods do not provide. Do not infer that benefit from implementation tests or non-scalar age geometry alone.

5. **Issue** — Exact computation for 32 fixed anchor impulses leaves unresolved sensitivity to the chosen source resolution and tile boundaries.
   **Where** — Sections 3.2–3.3, Proposition 3's fixed-endpoint/fixed-anchor qualifications, and the frozen `num_anchors=32` setting.
   **Why it matters** — “Exact” is correctly restricted to the finite anchor measure. Propagation visits all observed holds, but a short interval without an anchor contributes no independently encoded source at that interval. Thus exact chronological propagation does not imply sufficient source resolution for short ancestry tracts. Changing anchor count, anchor placement, tile endpoints, or sample set is outside the proven invariance contract. The data are clipped into eight tiles per parent, so dependence on tile edges can affect predictions even while redundant record subdivision leaves them invariant.
   **What would address it** — Freeze and report anchor-resolution and placement sensitivity, and evaluate different tile lengths or overlapping/context-extended tiles with common scoring coordinates. Stratify performance by short tracts and proximity to tile edges. Quantify the fraction of physical intervals or genomic mass represented by anchor-containing graphs. Present the subdivision theorem as a representation contract, and establish the practical resolution needed for prediction empirically rather than implying continuous-source accuracy or anchor-refinement invariance.

6. **Issue** — The ancestry study does not yet distinguish a generally useful age geometry from a prior tailored to one demographic simulator and reference panel.
   **Where** — Section 2.3's fixed scales of 350 and 1400 generations, Section 5.2's `AncientEurope_4A21` setting, and `docs/age_interface.md` qualification.
   **Why it matters** — The age transform is a defensible observed-input hypothesis, not direct evidence that old genealogical paths are misleading for this target. The qualification checks only five adjacent graphs from one training parent per input kind, and explicitly measures geometry rather than ancestry agreement. Different random seeds under the same demographic model measure simulation variation, not demographic generalization. The independently simulated Q8 and Q64 cohorts also cannot isolate the effect of increasing query count through a paired comparison. Relate inputs use observed phased variants, specified sample ages and a declared map, with simulated ancestral polarization, leaving important reconstruction and panel uncertainty untested.
   **What would address it** — Justify the fixed age scales before confirmatory evaluation and test scale sensitivity. Evaluate independent demographic settings, reference composition and density, and perturbations or alternative plausible reconstructions of topology and age. Subsample different query sizes from the same held-out parents when testing interface-size effects. Report per-class, calibration and tract-boundary metrics alongside NLL and accuracy. Keep any biological interpretation narrower than the simulation and reconstruction conditions actually evaluated.

7. **Issue** — The cross-domain forecasting protocol does not yet specify how the proposed two-sided anchor architecture becomes a leakage-free forecasting model.
   **Where** — Section 5.2, Equations (11) and (16), and the supplied Chickenpox configurations.
   **Why it matters** — A bidirectional kernel can legitimately aggregate a fully observed 52-week history. It cannot ingest the target week or later observations. The frozen Chickenpox boundary configuration describes earlier one-sided memory/control adaptations, not a direct frozen comparison of the new anchor model. Moreover, the inspected anchor implementation admits readouts only inside its supplied half-open tile, so forecasting beyond the history requires an explicit decoder or layout adaptation. The fixed 20-county graph can test temporal applicability, but supplies no evidence for changing anonymous interiors or integrated genealogical age geometry. The graph-SPDE work [2111.08524v3] also motivates a substantive covariance comparator rather than a citation-only comparison.
   **What would address it** — Freeze an anchor forecasting configuration specifying history-only sources, anchor masses and coordinates, forecast decoding, available graph information, and the relation to the ancestry model. Demonstrate the causal information boundary and report rolling chronological performance with the test block reserved until selection is fixed. Train the stated seasonal, persistence, ridge and appropriate graph-kernel comparators under disclosed protocols. Label results from earlier boundary adaptations separately, and limit this dataset's claim to the aspects of the proposed method it actually tests.

8. **Issue** — Cached geometry and mathematically bounded messages do not yet establish practical scaling or numerical reliability in the intended regimes.
   **Where** — Section 3.3's cost statement, Section 4.1's finite-arithmetic policy, and the audit's bounded Q64 checks.
   **Why it matters** — The online action has dense quadratic query cost, while Schur preparation, eigensystems and cache storage are separate costs. Existing checks at Q64 are useful but cannot establish a scalable ancestry method for substantially larger panels or higher tree density. Positive damping gives strictly positive row mass in exact arithmetic, but finite-rate exponentials can underflow, and the implementation rejects rather than repairs invalid mass. Bounded messages do not bound gradients through normalization, learned rates, gates, or the full predictor. The log/softplus comparison is therefore an optimization study requiring more than matching initial effective rates.
   **What would address it** — Report end-to-end preparation, storage, transfer, training and inference costs across query count, graph count, anchor count and branch count, together with accuracy tradeoffs. Include minimum row masses, rejection/failure rates, effective duration-times-decay distributions, and selected-rate/mixture and gradient diagnostics over independent runs. Document the tested numeric precision and coordinate units. Define how failed runs enter comparisons before observing test outcomes, and avoid interpreting an initial-output match as equivalent optimization dynamics.

### Minor concerns

- Equations (3)–(4) introduce `F_g^Q` and `U_g^R`, whereas the anchor construction starts from `U^0_{Q,a}` and `U^0_{R,a}` and applies further projections. Clarify whether the first pair is used, illustrative, or redundant, and distinguish encoder depth from kernel depth consistently.
- State whether genomic coordinates are measured in base pairs or normalized parent coordinates, the corresponding rate units, and how the convention changes across sequence lengths and forecasting. The inspected source preparation normalizes genomic positions by sequence length.
- Define the exact training loss, treatment of unequal retained coordinate counts after SNP clipping, calibration metrics and primary metric aggregation in the final paper, not only in receipts.
- The selected 47-feature bank, reference-role construction, ancestry census time and class semantics need a compact reproducible specification. Describe handling of disconnected, zero-response and equal-age cases as well as rejection rules.
- Keep the PSD claim explicitly attached to the unnormalized conditional block kernel. The normalized reference-inclusive action and nonlinear predictor are different objects, as the implementation manifest correctly records.
- Distinguish paper-equation adaptations from exact baseline reproductions in the final tables, provide runnable configurations and frozen artifacts, and disclose any relevant conflicts and data-use constraints. The present simulated ancestry and licensed county data do not themselves create a demonstrated ethics blocker.

### Verdict

**Major revision**. The preliminary construction is mathematically coherent, but stronger mechanism attribution, a locked independent evaluation and direct evidence for the anchor architecture are required before it can support a full AAAI-level research article.
