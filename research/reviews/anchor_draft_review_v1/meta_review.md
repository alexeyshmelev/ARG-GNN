# Preliminary methods-draft meta-review

This review concerns the frozen four-page methods/protocol draft, not a completed empirical submission. Available fresh-context Codex agents replaced the skill's Claude launcher. The reviewers worked independently from the same frozen manuscript and evidence. All criticism is retained. Final-paper review will use new independent context and completed evidence.

## Per-reviewer verdicts

- **alfa** — Major revision: The coherent mathematics needs direct anchor evidence, sharper contribution and robustness beyond one simulator and predecessor studies.
- **bravo** — Major revision: Fresh independent parent-level confirmation is the highest priority after extensive reuse of four validation parents.
- **charlie** — Major revision: Stronger mechanism attribution, locked independent evaluation and direct anchor results are needed for a full research article.

## Common concerns

1. **Independent confirmation and statistical unit.** All reviewers identify the four reused validation parents and one-seed studies as exploratory selection evidence. Bravo's strongest formulation is “Adding training seeds on those same parents does not create more independent evaluation parents.” Freeze the primary endpoint, principal paired contrasts, tuning budget, admission rules, practical effect/precision target and multiplicity policy before independent tests. Keep parent variability and training-seed variability separate.

2. **Contribution relative to established methods.** All reviewers find the construction plausible and its mathematics coherent, while distinguishing a correct synthesis from a substantial contribution. Charlie states that “correctness of these inherited properties alone does not establish an AAAI-level contribution.” Compare actual formulations of Kron/GNN reduction, dynamic graph kernels, GraphSSM, MP-SSM and GST against the fixed-interface/fixed-source design. A material predictive, robustness or computational advantage must come from evidence.

3. **Mechanism and local-capacity controls.** All reviewers explain that scalar, raw diagonal and gain-matched diagonal controls change different information paths, and depth adds capacity. Alfa notes that “Recording their count does not separate an advantage of repeated kernel context from an advantage of extra local nonlinear capacity.” Add matched local residual and noncontextual-source depth controls, same-anchor temporal/attention comparisons and simple observed-reference smoothing. Scope each contrast to the information paths it changes.

4. **Age geometry, selected scales and external validity.** All reviewers separate valid resistance mathematics from unproved ancestry benefit. The five adjacent graphs per source are a correlated label-free qualification sample. Complete matched age/scalar-age/plain comparisons and full-cohort geometry summaries. Prespecify scale selection and include meaningful demographic, reference-panel and reconstruction perturbations. A paired query-subsampling study is needed for a query-count claim.

5. **Finite anchors and artificial tile boundaries.** All reviewers distinguish exact finite-anchor action from continuous-source integration, anchor-refinement invariance or tiling robustness. Bravo notes that “Eight separate windows can create artificial boundaries for a method whose purpose is genomic context.” Test anchor count/placement, source coverage, common readouts under alternative/context-extended tiles, and short-tract/edge effects. Declare physical coordinate and rate units.

6. **Direct cross-domain adaptation.** All reviewers find that the frozen Chickenpox boundary adapter evaluates a predecessor, rather than the repeated fixed-anchor model. Alfa states that “A forecast from the earlier adapter cannot directly establish that this new mechanism transfers across domains.” Implement and freeze a history-only anchor adaptation, or keep that study as predecessor evidence. Evaluate seasonal, persistence, ridge, neural and appropriately scoped covariance controls with temporal dependence respected.

7. **End-to-end scaling and finite precision.** All reviewers ask for dense-interface scaling, Schur/eigensystem/cache cost, minimum row mass and numerical rejection diagnostics. Charlie emphasizes that “Bounded messages do not bound gradients through normalization, learned rates, gates, or the full predictor.” Report fitted-rate, mass, gradient and precision behavior across meaningful interface/tree/anchor sizes, including all failed runs in the experiment record.

8. **Reproducible paper and artifact specification.** The reviewers recognize strong provenance but need exact features, reference definitions, essential training/reconstruction settings, units and runnable artifacts in the paper or supplement. Bravo's formulation is “Records retain does not tell a reader where to obtain those records.” A public code URL alone does not replace a complete release and end-to-end recipe.

## Unique concerns

- **charlie** explicitly requests source-only versus propagation-only age interventions, separately changing the QR forcing block and QQ generator while preserving matching ones normalization. This distinguishes structural propagation from changed reference evidence.
- **bravo** provides the finite-sample illustration that four favorable independent parent signs still give a one-sided sign-test probability of1/16. This is support for the shared statistical concern rather than a required test or a new significance claim.
- **alfa** stresses that broader deployment claims require uncertainty in sample ages, phasing and maps, and that real-data application without ground truth cannot establish accuracy. These extend the shared external-validity concern.

## Ranking

1. **charlie** offers the most concrete mechanism interventions and identifies the predecessor-versus-anchor forecasting mismatch.
2. **bravo** makes the confirmatory statistical problem precise and connects attribution, public reproducibility and experiment design.
3. **alfa** supplies the broadest and most useful external-validity and numerical/scaling checklist. Its central concerns largely overlap the others.

No reviewer identifies a fatal algebra error or a false first-method claim. The ranking measures usefulness of revisions, not scientific authority.

## Verdict synthesis

**Major revision.** The panel unanimously regards the formal construction as coherent under its assumptions. The present snapshot has no completed anchor comparison, independent ancestry test or established distinctive benefit. The priorities are to finish the exploratory factorial, add controls that distinguish contextual exchange from local capacity/reference forcing, sharpen the contribution, then freeze and run fresh parent-level confirmation. Anchor/tile sensitivity, a direct forecasting adaptation, meaningful shifts and full cost/numerical reporting are needed before broader claims. Passing implementation tests supports correctness and does not resolve these empirical concerns.
