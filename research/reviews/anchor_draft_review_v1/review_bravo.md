### Summary

The strongest version of this paper is a coherent construction for learning over tree sequences whose sampled individuals persist while anonymous ancestors change. Its three concrete claims are that integrated age resistance preserves electrical subdivision equivalence, that chronological finite-anchor propagation supplies a symmetric PSD conditional kernel and bounded normalized messages, and that repeated anchor updates preserve the stated representation invariances. The propositions appear correct under their explicit assumptions, and the inspected implementation agrees with the essential source, propagation and normalization definitions. The proposed empirical contribution is narrower: this combination might provide useful ancestry context beyond existing graph, kernel and sequence models. The supplied frozen evidence does not yet establish that proposition. This is a preliminary methods/protocol review using the supplied skill-adapted framework, not an assessment of a completed empirical article. My stance is to take the mathematical construction seriously and press hard on how the future experiment will earn a reliable belief update.

### Major concerns

1. **Issue [Novelty]: The construction has a plausible contribution, but the draft does not yet specify which new capability or empirical proposition distinguishes this combination from its established ingredients.**

   **Where:** Section 1, Sections 3–4, and the absent completed anchor comparison.

   **Why it matters:** The attribution is substantially better than a generic “first graph state-space kernel” narrative. The draft acknowledges Kron reduction, graph neural reduction, temporal graph SSMs, dynamic graph kernels and graph SPDE covariance. Nonseparable graph covariance is already the territory of Nikitin et al. [2111.08524v3], space-time reconstruction on dynamic graphs is covered by Romero et al. [1612.03615v2], and temporal graph state-space modeling is the subject of GraphSSM [2406.00943v2]. Message-Passing State-Space Models [2505.18728v2] and Graph Set Transformer [2606.05116v1] also make generic claims about graph/sequence integration and repeated local/contextual computation unavailable as the novelty here. These inspected descriptions do not establish equivalence with this exact construction, and I found no demonstrably false attribution or explicit “first” claim. But a correct assembly of known components needs a clear reason for an AAAI reader to update. The propositions certify an implementation contract. They do not by themselves show that changing boundary operators supply useful memory.

   **What would address it:** State one precise candidate contribution, such as useful prior-query transfer across changing genealogies at physical genomic distances while retaining both subdivision invariances. Add a compact comparison of which cited methods support the persistent sampled interface, chronological noncommuting actions, finite anchor sources, clamped references and the two invariance contracts. Establish any claimed mathematical distinction rather than assuming it from the combination. Then tie the principal experiment to that specific proposition. A negative empirical result could still be informative if it sharply tests this inductive bias.

2. **Issue [Evidence — distinguishing tests, baselines and ablations]: The factorial controls are a good start, but they cannot yet uniquely attribute a gain to graph-conditioned propagation or kernel depth.**

   **Where:** Section 5.1, the nine-model plain pilot configuration, and the earlier boundary/common-input studies in evidence.json.

   **Why it matters:** The scalar control changes diagonal decay as well as cross-query transfer, which the final draft correctly admits. Raw diagonal keeps the decay diagonal but changes gain. Gain-matched diagonal retains instantaneous cross-query source mixing and matches only homogeneous continuous-forcing gain, not finite-anchor messages under changing operators. None is an all-purpose matched null. Likewise, depth two adds projections, a gate and normalization as well as another context exchange. An improvement could come from extra nonlinear capacity, reference-feature quality, different smoothing bandwidths, or rate optimization. The graph-local encoder already combines spatial evidence, so “scalar” is not a graph-free model.

   The earlier oracle Q64 boundary study, inferred common-input study, ancient common-input study and architecture controls concern different or earlier models. They provide useful development context, including no established overall boundary-flow advantage, but no completed experiment evaluates the new anchor prototype in the frozen snapshot. Oracle Q8 and independently simulated oracle Q64 scores are not paired evidence for scaling. The fixed-topology Chickenpox setting can test forecasting applicability and geographic dependence, but cannot distinguish the value of changing-tree order. The completed GP comparator is explicitly a stationary graph-OU adaptation, not a reproduction of Nikitin et al.

   **What would address it:** Add a depth/capacity-matched local residual control and a Transformer or independent temporal model using the same physical anchors, reference evidence and readouts. Report all three propagation controls with their separate causal interpretations. Compare age geometry with repeated-plain and matched scalar-age branches. Use controlled order/duration interventions, or a constructed task with a known dependence on prior query transfer, to test the chronological mechanism while keeping current sources comparable. In real ancestry data, include strong simple observed-reference predictors with calibrated temporal smoothing. Tune important baselines with an explicit comparable search budget and inspect learning curves before interpreting poor scores. One clear distinguishing experiment would carry more weight than a long table of architecture variants.

3. **Issue [Evidence — pre-specification, alternative explanations and cherry-picking]: Age-dependent geometry is mathematically qualified, but its biological utility and the chosen geometry bank remain hypotheses.**

   **Where:** Section 2.3, the age qualification described in the integration audit and pilot note, and the absent completed age-factorial study.

   **Why it matters:** The derivative of the stated potential is the positive resistance weight, endpoint differences telescope, and the energy minimization argument supports the one-quarter-to-one Loewner bounds. I found no error in those claims. They do not establish that downweighting older edges helps identify census-lineage ancestry. The scales 350 and 1400 generations and amplitude 3 are consequential choices without a biological or model-selection rationale in the draft. The observed qualification uses five adjacent graphs from one training parent per source. That is a small, correlated convenience sample, not evidence about a panel. Its strict-350 reference grounding is sparse: one of 64 queries in each sampled oracle graph, and zero or one of 64 in the sampled inferred graphs. This is a warning about evidence availability, not a performance result.

   There are no displayed qualitative ancestry examples to accuse of hand-picking. However, the adjacent-graph qualification must not later become the representative illustration of why age conditioning works. An alternative explanation for any eventual age-bank gain is additional effective decay scales or a favorable global attenuation, rather than biologically meaningful relative geometry. The present freeze also does not establish that every design choice was fixed before earlier development feedback.

   **What would address it:** Keep the qualification explicitly label-free and preliminary. Measure geometry residuals and reference coverage over a predeclared random or exhaustive parent-level sample. Give the provenance and rationale for age scales, amplitude and anchor count, and label any choices informed by previous pilots as exploratory. Complete the repeated-plain/age/scalar-age comparisons with the same encoder and branch capacity. Evaluate sensitivity to inferred age errors, panel composition and ancestry horizons, including conditions where attenuation is expected to hurt. Choose future qualitative windows by a declared random or exhaustive rule and retain failures.

4. **Issue [Limitations]: The manuscript understates the distinction between a representation-invariance guarantee and robustness to the scientific choices that determine the finite operator.**

   **Where:** Sections 3.2–3.3 and Proposition 3, with limitations scattered rather than consolidated.

   **Why it matters:** The subdivision theorem is appropriately conditional on unchanged tile endpoints, anchors, sample partition and observed features. It does not cover changing the tiling, refining anchors, changing the reference panel, or changing inferred topology and ages. Eight separate windows can create artificial boundaries for a method whose purpose is genomic context. Exact finite-anchor summation does not guarantee adequate source resolution: a short genealogy containing useful reference evidence can influence propagators while receiving no source anchor. The bound on M is an aggregation bound, not a stability or uncertainty guarantee for learned projections, LayerNorm, logits or gradients.

   I do not think the stated theorem is false. The limitation is that its practical importance is unmeasured, while fixed anchor count, finite-domain edge effects, dense query scaling and reconstruction error may dominate actual use. Strictly positive edge resistance and numerical mass rejection are also real admissibility conditions. A positive exact denominator need not survive arbitrarily large learned decay in floating-point arithmetic.

   **What would address it:** Add a concise limitations section that names the finite-anchor target, lack of an anchor-refinement guarantee, sensitivity to tile endpoints/panel changes, reconstructed-tree uncertainty, and dense preparation/propagation cost. Report anchor-count and tile-length sensitivity, performance near artificial boundaries, excluded or rejected inputs, mass minima/failure rates, and total preparation plus training cost. Declare coordinate units and rate scaling. Retain the correct separation between the PSD kernel, rectangular reference forcing, normalized messages and the nonlinear predictor.

5. **Issue [Reproducibility]: The auditable repository is stronger than the manuscript's reproducibility specification, which is not yet enough for an independent reconstruction of the intended study.**

   **Where:** Sections 2.2 and 5.2, and absent public artifact link and complete training/data specification.

   **Why it matters:** Review access includes source, configurations, hash-bound artifacts, seeds and an implementation audit. The inspected manuscript and frozen plain configuration hashes agree with their supplied receipts. This is credible provenance evidence. It is not evidence that code, datasets or selected weights are publicly released. There is no headline empirical figure to replicate yet. A competent student could reconstruct the broad architecture from the equations but would still need the exact 47-feature definitions, reference-summary conventions, coordinate normalization, reconstruction/export settings, model initialization and training schedule. These are material choices, especially when informative engineered reference features are present. “Records retain” does not tell a reader where to obtain those records.

   **What would address it:** Provide a stable release or anonymous review artifact, a runnable end-to-end recipe, dataset/license/provenance details, exact feature formulas and preprocessing, frozen configs/environment, seed scheme, and eventually selected checkpoints and parent-level predictions. Put essential hyperparameters and units in the paper or supplement rather than relying on private code. Specify the Chickenpox adaptation's source roles, anchors, causal history boundary and normalization, since a bidirectional historical kernel is acceptable only when every source is available before the forecast target. Preserve the existing explicit adaptation/reproduction distinctions.

6. **Issue [Evidence — statistical rigour]: Four repeatedly reused validation parents and one training seed cannot support a reliable confirmatory claim about the new architecture, however many windows, haplotypes or coordinates are scored.**

   **Where:** Section 5.2, evidence.json's truthful scope notes, and the absent final ancestry evaluation and uncertainty protocol.

   **Why it matters:** Splitting independent simulation parents before windowing is correct and prevents the obvious leakage. Selecting checkpoints by mean parent-validation NLL is also clearly stated. But the same four validation parents then participate in checkpoint, architecture and protocol development. Their score is selection evidence. It is not a fresh estimate of generalization. Genomic positions, queries and eight tiles from a parent are dependent. Counting them as independent observations would manufacture precision. Even four consistently favorable independent parent differences yield only p=1/16=0.0625 in a one-sided sign test. Adding training seeds on those same parents does not create more independent evaluation parents.

   The supplement honestly reports one-seed exploratory pilots and no generated ancestry test, and there are no p-values or significance claims to contest. Parent-level values are available, which is useful, but the draft gives no seed dispersion, intervals, power rationale, confirmatory contrast or multiplicity policy. Weak .01 < p < .05 exploratory findings would not persuade me. The chronological Chickenpox targets also overlap in their 52-week histories and share county dynamics, so treating county-week errors as independent would produce a second version of the same problem. The five-graph age sample is still less informative about population variation.

   **What would address it:** Before confirmation, freeze the primary metric, the small set of contrasts, tuning budget, data admission rules and analysis. Generate a fresh independently seeded ancestry test cohort large enough to resolve a practically meaningful parent-level effect, keeping it outside further development. Repeat training seeds and report their variability separately from variation across evaluation parents. Report paired parent-level differences and defensible uncertainty intervals, retaining all runs and informative negative outcomes. If testing significance, predeclare multiplicity handling and use the supplied stringent exploratory/confirmatory standards rather than celebrating marginal p-values. For Chickenpox, specify rolling evaluation and dependence-aware intervals or block analysis, plus seasonal and persistence errors. This is the highest-priority requirement for converting the sound protocol candidate into a full research claim.

### Minor concerns

- **Narrative — claims and cohesion:** The three mathematical/construction claims above form one theme. I found no grab-bag problem. The empirical proposition is explicitly a question in Section 1. Keep this distinction in the final article.
- **Narrative — abstract check:** “This working methods document accompanies ongoing validation-only architecture comparisons” is an honest scope statement. “Exact finite anchor actions” is qualified by Section 3.3's “exact action for the predeclared finite anchor measure.” I found no abstract superiority or first-method claim. Change “Matched controls separate age geometry, kernel depth, query coupling and positive-rate optimization” to a prospective statement that the controls test these factors, since completed attribution is absent.
- **Correctness check:** Proposition 1's covariance equation keeps P=I even when symmetric positive generators do not commute. Proposition 2 uses the Metzler property and a matching reference-inclusive ones scan. Proposition 3 follows from telescoping resistance, fixed features/anchors and the semigroup identity. I found no blocking mathematical error in those arguments. Do not expand their scope to the normalized operator being a symmetric Gram matrix.
- **Evidence — implementation checks:** The supplied independent audit reports 109 targeted CPU tests and real inferred-tile forward/backward checks. The inspected scan uses inclusive forward and exclusive reverse actions, with readouts excluded from sources. These address numerical/self-counting and invariance correctness. They supply no statistical evidence of ancestry benefit. No new experiments were run for this review.
- **Evidence — quality and quantity check:** The case currently consists of a useful construction, implementation checks and earlier exploratory pilots, with no completed anchor result. It is not yet carried by one compelling empirical experiment. Concern 2 specifies the missing distinguishing tests. No empirical result was inferred from the number of passing tests.
- **Presentation — precision:** “Fixed kernel evidence” and “source-conditioned observed common-ancestor times” in Section 2.2 need concrete definitions. State what is known about reference labels, and define “ancestry horizon” before using it to motivate the age bank. The introduction otherwise gives a reasonable orientation to the persistent-sample/changing-ancestor problem.
- **Presentation — notation:** S denotes the interface, S with indices denotes source signals, and S in the cost formula denotes branch count. H denotes both embeddings/latent count in nearby notation while the kernel is calligraphic H. Rename the counts. Explain how F from Equation 3 connects to the later q source, since it is not subsequently used as written.
- **Presentation — figures:** There are no empirical figures or tables in this draft, so there are no existing axes, units, legends, color scales or captions to assess. A future schematic should show Q/R roles, fixed anchors, changing holds and the readout/source separation. Future empirical plots should identify independent parent counts, interval units, uncertainty and whether values are validation or test.
- **Limitations — honesty check:** The finite-anchor qualification, conditional invariance statement, numerical rejection policy and validation-only scope are substantive and honest. There is no existing dedicated limitations section to call a fig leaf. Concern 4 identifies what remains insufficiently visible.

### Belief update

I updated substantially toward the narrower engineering proposition that this particular finite-anchor construction can combine chronological noncommuting graph propagation, clamped observed reference evidence and nonlinear query updates without breaking the two stated subdivision invariances. The covariance and positivity arguments make that plausible, and the inspectable implementation/audit reinforce it. I made essentially no positive update toward better ancestry inference, biologically appropriate age weighting, or a distinctive AAAI-level empirical contribution: the frozen snapshot contains no completed anchor comparison. The key next update must come from a predeclared, independently evaluated effect across parents, not another favorable selection score on the same four validation simulations.

### Verdict

**Major revision** — the preliminary construction is mathematically credible, but the statistical-rigour gap in concern 6 must be closed with fresh parent-level confirmation before it can support a full AAAI-level research claim.
