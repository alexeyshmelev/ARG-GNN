## Weakening, leakage and convergence verdicts

- **Confirmed departures requiring disclosure:** ARGMix current-release15/no-ancestor input versus paper25+3; common-data rosters/maps/errors/training diversity and reference eligibility; AP bounded sampler/BAA path6 shortfalls and derived endpoint; reduced-European ARGformer task/microbatch/final-checkpoint adaptations; release ARGformer.30/uniform objective versus paper.05/frequency weighting; American evaluator/ploidy reconstruction and FLARE marker projection.
- **No local architecture/epoch truncation established in completed native traces:** ARGMix uses its own early-stop rule; AP completes5; reduced-European ARGformer completes5+1 with full architecture/full contrastive batch. This is narrower than claiming no possible weakening. It does not prove that the paper's intended strongest baseline, equivalent feature information, effective data budget or convergence was reached.
- **No query-truth fitting leakage found in the reviewed execution chain:** supervised European training labels and ARGMix inner VAL checkpoint selection are explicit intended uses. AP/ARGformer/FLARE prediction sealing precedes external scoring. ARGformer includes held-out-parent observations transductively without query ancestry; this is not inductive cross-parent generalization. The repeatedly reused final development VAL metrics remain selection-biased evidence.
- **Unresolved, not exonerated:** exact publication settings/evaluators/checkpoints, sensitivity of reference restrictions/sampler deficit, modern legacy-numerical differences and optimization saturation. No direction or magnitude of accuracy impact is inferred without an experiment. No intention to weaken a competitor is inferred.

The appropriate manuscript conclusion is a descriptive comparison of these named pipelines under their actual contracts, with visible unresolved scientific TODOs. Any equal-strength, architecture-superiority or exact-publication claim needs additional evidence.
