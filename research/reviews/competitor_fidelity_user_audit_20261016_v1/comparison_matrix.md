All European full-panel values are repeatedly used development VAL results with one training seed. Eight simulations are the biological replication unit; SNPs, windows and haplotypes are dependent observations. AP and ARGMix do not have equal checkpoint-selection opportunities. The candidate GNN also consumes observed SNPs. Common support allows descriptive pipeline comparison, not proof of equal information, equal training opportunity or architecture superiority.
