# Ordered stable-tip commutators: priority and prediction decision

2026-10-15. Independent source/literature assessment. No implementation or execution proposal is admitted by this note.

## Decision

**Reject the second/third-order diffusion commutator as the next claim of a novel graph-collection operator. Do not allocate a new predictive fit to it on the present justification.** It is mathematically sound, efficient in expanded marginal-tree size, and potentially a useful feature. Its essential mechanism has direct temporal-network priority, while its third-order extension is classical Magnus/BCH/log-signature machinery. Restricting it to clade operators and implementing it in Torch/PyG does not establish novelty. I did not find a verified paper implementing the exact proposed learned ARG feature, which is a narrower statement than finding a new architecture.

The biological case is weaker than the algebraic case. H042 already supplies exact local changed-clade information to full-tree propagation and the full genomic BiGRU. H043's true foreign-state arm exceeds its own-state control by only 0.0153 percentage points and trails H042. This does not disprove all multistep interactions, but it supplies no positive predictive reason to favor another topology-transfer mechanism. A tree-hold commutator also adds no observation inside a hold, so it cannot by itself repair the recorded missing-boundary-candidate limitation. H044 directly tests SNP resolution and has no accuracy result in the inspected record. Its outcome should determine whether a residual biological problem remains before another architecture is selected.

## The precise candidate that was assessed

Use the same persistent order of all H sampled tips at every locus. For clade C let E_C average values within C and act as identity outside C. The earlier assessment correctly identifies G_t = sum_C r_(t,C)(E_C-I), r >= 0, as an established hierarchical Laplacian generator. Let S_t = exp(d_t G_t), with physical length d_t and current-observation-conditioned, state-independent rates. Each S_t is a symmetric stochastic Euclidean contraction. Its exact linear-time tree action is available through the existing assessment's tree means/contrasts or prefix/range-add construction. Learning the rates does not change the operator's mathematical ancestry.

For adjacent operators A=S_t, B=S_(t+1), and one common leaf probe Z, the proposed second-order feature is

    K2 Z = (BA-AB) Z.

A three-tree feature can be

    K3 Z = [C,[B,A]] Z
         = (CBA-CAB-BAC+ABC) Z,  C=S_(t+2).

These detect incompatible clade partitions using shared leaf identity, without pretending that internal nodes correspond. For a simultaneous tip permutation P, every S becomes P S P^T and Z becomes PZ, so both outputs become P times the old output. Arbitrary internal-node permutations leave the clades and action unchanged. This is tip-permutation equivariance and internal-label invariance, conditional on the upstream stable sample-index contract. It does not confer invariance to biological tip misalignment or topology error. Unary-equivalent clades require preserving total rates, rather than independently predicting different rates after inserting unary nodes.

There are useful degeneracy checks. Constant probes give zero commutators because every S preserves the constant vector. Global-mean readout discards them because 1^T K2 = 1^T K3 = 0. A is symmetric and B is symmetric, so K2 is skew-symmetric and z^T K2 z = 0 for any real scalar probe z. Query-resolved K2 Z can still be nonzero. A shared scalar self-energy readout would accidentally erase the claimed mechanism. The common root-averaging component commutes with every clade operator.

Genomic reversal exchanges A and B and changes K2's sign. A correct ancestry predictor should reverse its locus outputs when observations reverse. It needs paired forward/backward branches or an explicit reversal-equivariant construction. Reversal symmetry does not make all ordered information useless, but an arbitrary orientation-odd score is not a biological ancestry signal. Squaring K2 retains a different, orientation-even incompatibility statistic. Adding ordinary third-order BCH terms does not automatically solve this distinction. The two-factor degree-three term [B-A,[B,A]] is unchanged when A and B are swapped, so calling that term an order detector would be wrong.

## Verified nearest prior and what each establishes

1. **Allen, Moore and Hebert-Dufresne, 2024, Compressing the chronology of a temporal network with graph commutators**, Physical Review Letters 132, 077402. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.077402. Primary arXiv v3: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11566v3. The primary abstract explicitly proposes combining successive network snapshots whose matrix commutators have the smallest dynamical effect. This is direct priority for graph-collection chronology, graph commutators, and their effect on propagation, rather than a loose analogy to static GNNs. It is not a learned ARG model or proof of this exact readout.
2. **Speidel, Klemm, Eguiluz and Masuda, 2016, Temporal interactions facilitate endemicity in the susceptible-infected-susceptible epidemic model**, New Journal of Physics 18, 073013. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/18/7/073013. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00859v2. The abstract identifies adjacency-matrix Lie commutators and their norm as determinants/predictors of temporal-versus-static dynamics. Thus commutator magnitude as graph-order information also predates the 2024 paper.
3. **Blanes, Casas, Oteo and Ros, 2009, The Magnus expansion and some of its applications**, Physics Reports 470, 151-238. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2008.11.001. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.5488. Second-order brackets and third-order nested brackets arise from the standard logarithm of time-ordered linear evolution. Truncating at degree three is not a new operator principle. The formulas above are algebraic derivations, not quoted experimental findings.
4. **Morrill, Salvi, Kidger, Foster and Lyons, Neural Rough Differential Equations for Long Time Series**, primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.08295v4. The abstract states that log-signatures summarize how small input intervals drive a CDE, with memory and speed motivations. **Choi and Park, Graph Neural Rough Differential Equations for Traffic Forecasting**, primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10909v2, explicitly extends log-signature-based temporal processing to a spatial graph framework. Neither abstract establishes equivalence to changing clade semigroups. They preclude a broad claim that adding learned low-order ordered signatures to graph processing is new. I verified primary records, not journal publication for these two papers.
5. The existing source audit verifies **Bendikov and Krupski, On the spectrum of the hierarchical Laplacian**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s11118-014-9409-6, primary https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.4883v2, as the exact hierarchical generator and cumulative ancestor-rate spectrum. GRAND and fast Haar neural filtering are additional neural precedents already assessed there. Cellular sheaf/gauge models are broader transport context, not needed for this rejection. I make no assertion that a sheaf layer is exactly the same operator.

The bounded search does not prove exhaustive absence of the exact leaf-clade implementation. The affirmative direct priorities above are sufficient to reject novelty from merely composing these ingredients. A new exact update algorithm exploiting shared tree edits with a proved complexity gain, or a new identifiable biological estimand with a prediction/recovery result, would be a different contribution. Neither is supplied by K2/K3.

## Feasibility, without a speed or memory-qualification claim

All H=952 tips must remain, including the 686 readout queries and the references that mediate clade averaging. Query-only propagation changes the operator. If a typical binary tree has N=2H-1=1903 nodes and T=1000 holds, a width-D=8 feature table uses T*H*D*4 = 30.464 MB in FP32. One tree action costs O(ND). Direct K2 uses four actions per boundary, about 60.9 million node-channel action units across the collection. Direct K3 uses twelve actions per triple, about 182.7 million units. These are arithmetic-structure estimates, not FLOPs, GPU timings or an admitted resource budget. Polytomies/unary nodes change N and must use actual source-authorized counts later.

No dense 952-by-952 operator is required. A dense bank of 1000 such FP32 matrices alone would occupy about 3.63 GB. Streaming three adjacent operators requires O((N+H)D) working feature space plus topology and the backbone. Keeping every action for autograd raises memory by its action count and internal buffers. Width-32 states multiply the stated feature bytes by four. Existing H038 memory evidence cannot certify a new fit. The approach is linear in materialized marginal trees, not proved linear in the compressed ARG or number of tree edits. Bounded operator norms give ||K2|| <= 2 and ||K3|| <= 4 for contractions, but do not preserve Markov positivity when adding a commutator residual. It must be described as a signed feature, not as a probability-preserving ancestry diffusion.

## What could falsify the narrow usefulness hypothesis if revived

The only plausible biological hypothesis is that reference-conditioned incompatible clade paths distinguish a query's source-affinity switch from unrelated topology changes. This could add information beyond two independent changed-clade flags. It is not an inferred recombination event, admixture boundary, ancestor identity, or evidence that genome position is evolutionary time. Inferred tree noise and reference-only recombinations can also produce large values. The current evidence does not justify saying a prediction gain is likely beyond H042/H044.

A future minimal fit would keep a frozen-choice H042/H044 architecture and all observations, add exactly the same 512 bias-free weights in each arm (32-to-8 probe and 8-to-32 residual), and compare (BA-AB)Z with the symmetric product (BA+AB)Z using the same four tree actions, parameter count, backbone updates, training parents, checkpoint schedule and selection rule. Rates/normalization and reversal handling must be identical and frozen before scoring. This pair asks whether antisymmetric interaction helps over equally costly two-tree mixing. It alone cannot attribute a gain specifically to chronology, since smoothing and signed contrast also differ.

A required same-checkpoint intervention would swap chronological factors while preserving each graph, duration, probe, evidence and local flags, and score reversal equivariance separately from arbitrary order destruction. A separately trained, geometry-preserving tip-alignment corruption control would test whether stable cross-tree identity matters. An independent first-order/change-feature matched arm is needed before claiming advantage over H042's local signal. Common first-order and symmetric features should be supplied to all arms if the scientific claim is the incremental value of K2. A degree-three model should be considered only after degree two has a positive mechanistic result, with its own matched budget.

Do not use the repeatedly selected eight development simulations as confirmatory evidence. Freeze the selected design and evaluate on new held-out biological replicates with paired uncertainty, span accuracy, boundary precision/recall/F1, and per-cohort effects. A gain disappearing after the symmetric/local controls, a reversal failure, no dependence on correct tip alignment, or no independent boundary/accuracy benefit falsifies the proposed mechanism or its practical value. None of these controls was run here. This note recommends completing the already-registered H044 resolution test rather than launching this candidate.
