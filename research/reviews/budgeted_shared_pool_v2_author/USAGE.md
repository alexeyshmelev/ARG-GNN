The only operational delta is three finite module names plus exact source/config pins for the two already sealed workers. New names are arggnn.native_argformer_tsinfer_fit_v5, arggnn.census_biological_fit_v1 and arggnn.census_biological_device_profile_v1. Native-v5 and Census-fit source/config hashes are fixed in policy; a registered job must bind those exact bytes and the config in worker_bindings. The unfinished Census device-profile source/config are not invented or globally pinned: the concrete reviewed plan must bind its exact module source, config/capability references and literal argv hash.

Eligibility is not scientific qualification or route compatibility. Actual CUDA UUID/VRAM/title/native-interpreter qualification is not performed by this author. Its sealed root admission still requires existing_root_controlled_exclusive_project_launcher with the pinned launch_gpu.py/resources.py contract. V2 explicitly rejects a native-v5 shared plan. Native v5 retains its unchanged standalone exclusive route; a separate narrowly reviewed native shared-provenance successor is required to share this pool.

Its worker requires an independent scientific-protocol review, numerically qualified source/runtime, actual full-tree GPU/output-VJP/backward profile evidence, launcher/watchdog and fit capabilities. It has no profile-only CLI. The separately authored census_biological_device_profile_v1 name is included for future exact plans; the source/config/capability and its scientific review are pending. The allowlist grants none of those worker authorities and produces no actual-profile or fitting claim.

All exact root plan/review/source/config/argv/reservation bindings, current global and all-own budget/free-space checks,2-physical-device limit, UUID/PID attribution, complete pending-CUDA memory reservations, FIFO, inherited FD/environment, process/log supervision, natural drain and cleanup are unchanged.
