**H045: bounded source and prior-art comparison — 15 October 2026**

H045 is a specific structural grouping and message-routing choice built from established operations. The checked literature establishes prior work for mean aggregation, low-rank bilinear interactions, cross-graph state exchange, graph pooling, and neural learning from ordered genealogical trees. This bounded comparison does not establish publication priority for H045's exact combination. It supplies no acceptance judgment, predictive result, or novelty promise.

The inspected H045 source groups occurrences of the same exact proper sampled-descendant set into maximal consecutive runs. An absent tree breaks the run. Roots and singleton clades are excluded, duplicate unary clades use the deepest representative, and every original graph vertex remains in both whole-tree sweeps. The first-sweep states are shared through a physical-span-weighted, leave-one-tree-out mean. A separate rank-eight residual is added alongside the complete learned H042 flag adapter before the second sweep. SNP features and the genomic BiGRU remain. This is structural membership derived from stable sampled-leaf identity, not internal-ancestor homology.

For episode E, define W=sum(j in E) w_j. At an eligible member i,

`m_i = sum(j in E, j!=i) [w_j/(W-w_i)] h_j`.

Thus the pool is exactly message passing on an off-diagonal, row-normalized weighted clique within each episode. Its implementation factors the aggregation through an episode sum and removes the recipient contribution, avoiding materializing that clique. Equivalently it is an auxiliary episode-factor pool/broadcast with recipient subtraction. This reduction is algebra from H045, not a claim made by a cited paper. The residual `O(Uh_i * Vm_i + Wh_i)` is low-rank bilinear pooling plus a linear term. Rank eight, 1,024 added weights, zero initial O, and matched AdamW are experimental/optimization choices.

The strongest novelty and biological risks are concrete:

1. **Contiguity is the specialized choice, not a new temporal operator.** Holding membership and state/weight pairs fixed, permuting members inside an episode only permutes the pool outputs. The pool has no direction-of-travel information within a run. Order affects where episodes start/end. The full predictor also has its inherited BiGRU, so this statement must not be generalized into permutation or reversal guarantees for the complete network.

2. **Exact descendant equality is weaker than ancestral identity or ancestry constancy.** Internal topology, times, mutations, and ancestry can change while an enclosing descendant set stays equal. Conversely, one inferred-tree error can fragment a long episode. Wong et al.'s [general ARG formalism, inheritance-edge definition and Fig. 1](https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC11373519), and Ralph et al.'s framework, distinguish persistent graph identity from locus-dependent descendants. H045 supplies neither a homology estimator nor a topology-error stability guarantee.

3. **Duration weighting does not make the leave-one-hold-out operator subdivision invariant.** With two equal-width members h1=0, h2=1, the first member receives context 1. Split its hold into two half-width copies, both with state 0. Either copy now receives context 2/3. Even with first-sweep states held fixed, equivalent duplication changes the pool. The global weighted mean is subdivision invariant, but excluding one member is not. This is an analytic limitation of the chosen estimand, not an implementation failure or an executed experiment. It matters when unrelated tree changes create extra holds inside an otherwise persistent clade.

4. **Leaving out a tree is not statistical independence.** Members share sampled haplotypes, correlated genealogical structure, and related observed evidence. Foreign-state averaging could help through generic smoothing, or blur genuine changes. An improvement over own-state would support the utility of foreign state in this architecture, but would not isolate the exact clade matching, contiguity rule, or duration weights as its cause.

5. **Prediction support stays at native holds.** The episode adapter supplies no new within-hold coordinates. It cannot itself create SNP-resolved ancestry boundaries. H044 is a descriptive comparison with different preparation/readout, not an equal-input test of episode pooling.

A testable, narrower methodological question is whether exact descendant-set persistence provides useful cross-locus reference-affinity context without persistent internal-node IDs, especially where local evidence is weak. The registered foreign-versus-own comparison is a valid first contrast for foreign latent-state use. It does not establish a new general GNN family or that this grouping is preferable to generic nearby context.

Future discriminating tests, requiring a separately frozen protocol, would compare correct episode routing with a clade-size/duration/eligibility-matched foreign-state reassignment, and with a distance-matched contiguous genomic window. Report how much the reassignment actually changes. A global equal-clade pool would test whether breaking on absence matters. Controlled tree error and artificial hold subdivision would test the two brittleness risks above. Evaluate accuracy, boundary/short-tract effects and weak-evidence strata on independent parents and replicated seeds. The registered 0.10 percentage-point accuracy and one percentage-point boundary-F1 criteria are practical rules, not significance, priority, or benchmark-superiority evidence.
