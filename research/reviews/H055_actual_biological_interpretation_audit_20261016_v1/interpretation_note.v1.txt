H055 actual biological interpretation audit — post-outcome
2026-10-16. Source/roster/cached-publication metadata only. No raw genotype, model prediction, inferred-tree or checkpoint payload read; no scientific execution.

Conclusion
A description of H055 as testing Denmark's initial Mesolithic-to-Neolithic/farming transition is incorrect. The actual earlier people are all labelled Early Neolithic (EN) in the original sample-context metadata; the available later contexts are Middle Neolithic B / Late Neolithic. Both groups have the broad original group label Denmark_Neolithic. “Earlier versus later Neolithic” is accurate. This correction neither changes the registered contrast nor makes its negative result a success.

These are ten distinct sites, not repeated samples from the same local communities. Archaeological period, country, date and source group label do not certify an individual's genetic ancestry. The differing wetland/burial contexts and geographic sampling also limit a population-wide temporal interpretation. No relatives listed is not proof of genetic independence.

What historical question these dates can address
The cached Population genomics of post-glacial western Eurasia (DOI10.1038/s41586-023-06865-0), paragraph14, places Anatolian-related farmer arrival in Denmark at “around5,900 bp”. Even the oldest earlier95% bound,5747 BP, is later. This panel therefore has no pre-farming/Mesolithic comparison group and cannot measure the introduction of agriculture.
The paper distinguishes that farmer transition from subsequent steppe-related expansion: paragraph6 discusses steppe expansion from approximately5,000 BP; paragraph17 describes two large turnovers in Denmark/southern Sweden about1,000 years apart; paragraph19 links Corded Ware-related groups to steppe ancestry and already admixed farmer ancestry. Its cited Danish companion is100 ancient genomes show repeated population turnovers in Neolithic Denmark, DOI10.1038/s41586-023-06862-3 (reference34). The companion's full text was not read in this audit.
The defensible framing is a finite-panel temporal-affinity comparison of selected Early Neolithic people with selected much later MN B/LN people across the broad farmer-to-steppe-associated demographic period. The panel does not directly sample the transition boundary, date migration, establish cultural membership for every person, or quantify population replacement.

Was positive f4 justified?
As a directional biological prior for farmer-associated earlier people versus steppe-enriched later people, positive f4(later,earlier;Sidelkino,Bar8) and f4(later,earlier;KK1,Bar8) was plausible: the cached paper describes EHG/CHG contributions to steppe-related ancestry (paragraphs6/18), while Bar8 is the preselected Anatolian proxy. Under the frozen product convention, positive means the later-minus-earlier change covaries with C-minus-Bar8; there is no biological reason to reverse this sign now.
But dates, EN/MN/LN labels and eight proxy annotations never establish that these exact five later people are uniformly steppe-enriched, nor that a continental pattern must appear in these three windows with these specific distal single-person contrasts. Sidelkino/KK1/Bar8 are proxies, not pure ancestral components or direct Yamnaya samples; f4 is not a mixture fraction. The earlier readiness statement that this pilot “replicates a documented Neolithic-to-steppe-associated temporal pattern” was too strong before actual finite-panel evidence. The expectation was a falsifiable extrapolation, not an already established result for this roster. If instead justified as initial neolithization, that justification was wrong: initial farmer arrival generally concerns increased Anatolian-related ancestry and this roster does not bracket it.

Actual result and limits
Root reports genuine completion and then, after H056 registration, opened H055 outcomes:14,432 common13-support SNPs; pooled f4=-0.00223403016679 and-0.00460966313238, with both contrasts negative on every fixed chromosome. This audit did not open result payloads or independently recompute those values.
Those signs contradict H055's fixed positive-direction prediction. They must remain a failed corroboration outcome. They do not by themselves demonstrate that later Denmark lacked steppe ancestry, overturn published population history, establish a causal historical reversal, validate ancestry accuracy, or prove an implementation/label error. With three windows and a small site-selected cohort, no significance claim or post-hoc population explanation follows from sign alone.
No new cohort, window, QC, proxy, direction, exclusion or ancestry label is selected. Model and genotype contrasts share source data/proxies; neither supplies independent empirical ancestry truth. H056 remains separately prospectively registered; this audit is explicitly post-outcome explanation and cannot be presented as preregistered evidence or used to redefine either experiment.
