# Residual biological errors from existing H045–H047 scores

Present-day base-pair accuracy remains about 96.8%, but only 22.6–23.2% of true ancestry changes are recovered. The available evidence supports a persistent transition-localization gap. It does not identify a new biological cause or justify an additional experimental condition.

All values below are from completed, already-scored receipts. Accuracy and boundary P/R/F1 are equal-parent means ± sample SD (ddof1), over eight shared development VAL parents unless stated. P/R/F1 use percentages; paired changes use percentage points. Boundary counts and marker-support fractions are pooled across parents. Cohort accuracy ranges span the three intervention-arm means and are not uncertainty intervals. The companion JSON preserves both arms, all seven groups and exact fractional values.

Present182 is sampled at age 0, 166 generations after its first mixture pulse. C4 means the ancestor's simulated source population at 350 generations BP, not the sample's cohort. The original simulation sets the four mixture times from the oldest samples of the corresponding ancient cohorts plus one generation. Sampling age, population, possible ancestry sources and tract complexity are therefore coupled. The table shows an association with cohort structure, not an age-only causal explanation. Ancient504 and All686 overlap the disjoint cohorts and must not be treated as independent replications.

H046 marker strata use the minimum usable SNP count on the two sides within the original focal hold. A zero means at least one flank has no usable SNP. All four bins have support in all eight parents. Accuracy differs by only 0.04054 pp from the lowest to highest bin and is not monotonic, while NLL decreases across these four bin means. These aggregate associations do not isolate marker density, show boundary dependence on marker support, or exclude a localized marker limitation. H045/H047 have no comparable saved marker strata.

The frozen score integrates exact intervals over clipped 100 bp support and uses a 1,000 bp boundary tolerance ([reporting definition](../../../research/reviews/H046_completed_comparison_root_20261015_v1/REPORT.md)). Each condition used 32 TRAIN/8 VAL parents, 2 Mb, 952 input haplotypes / 686 queries, seed 101 and 30 added FP32 passes (960 updates) per arm. Selection and reporting share VAL. Parent SD is not optimizer-seed uncertainty, and there is no new outer-test evidence here.

**No justified additional condition.** H045's foreign-episode gain and H046's foreign-flank gain remain below their registered practical accuracy criterion. H047 risk balancing worsened accuracy/NLL despite a small boundary-F1 increase. The prior H038 continuity/within-hold recommendations already have successors; H048 marker-grid reset-chain, H049 order-2 cut-memory, and the donor-identity/availability condition have existing owners/contracts. Repeating or renaming them would not answer a distinct question supported by this extraction. H045–H047 tract-length response remains unmeasured in the saved diagnostics.

This review opens no fresh truth, probability arrays or checkpoints and runs no scorer, fit or perturbation. It adds no registration, code change, job, installation, guard or Git operation. [Extracted metrics](metrics.json) and [source hashes](sources.json) preserve provenance.
