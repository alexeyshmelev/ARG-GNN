# Verified prior-work boundary

Verification used publisher-deposited Crossref records and primary abstract pages on2026-10-15. Matching-cluster/GRF/EvolveGCN technical summaries below were checked against deposited abstracts; TGN/TMD against arXiv abstract metadata; GW against its PMLR abstract. RF title/authors/year/DOI were checked against Crossref, without claiming a full-text audit. This is a focused prior-work check, not a systematic review or proof of novelty. No PDF was downloaded.

| Verified reference | Relevance and limit |
|---|---|
| D.F. Robinson and L.R. Foulds (1981), *Comparison of phylogenetic trees*, Mathematical Biosciences. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0025-5564%2881%2990043-2) | Established tree comparison. Exact observed clade/split agreement is not a new object; the candidate is RF-like at its comparison stage, not a new distance metric. |
| Damian Bogdanowicz and Krzysztof Giaro (2013), *On a matching distance between rooted phylogenetic trees*, International Journal of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2478/amcs-2013-0050) | Closest verified cluster-level comparison prior: Matching Cluster distance uses clusters and minimum-weight perfect bipartite matching. H043's deterministic leaf-conditioned, possibly many-to-many contingency is different, but does not make cluster matching itself novel. |
| Martin R. Smith (2020), *Information theoretic generalized Robinson–Foulds metrics for comparing phylogenetic trees*, Bioinformatics. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa614) | Generalized RF pairs similar splits and scores their similarity. Rules out broad novelty claims for soft/partial tree-structure correspondence. H043 neither optimizes that pairing nor estimates a new tree metric. Cite the2020 original DOI, not the separate2021 record. |
| Aldo Pareja et al. (2020), *EvolveGCN: Evolving Graph Convolutional Networks for Dynamic Graphs*, AAAI. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v34i04.5984) | Handles dynamic graphs and changing node sets by evolving GCN parameters. Anonymous/changing nodes are an established problem. It does not establish this particular clade incidence or ancestral correspondence. |
| Emanuele Rossi et al. (2020), *Temporal Graph Networks for Deep Learning on Dynamic Graphs*. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10637) | Generic memory/graph operators over timed events establish dynamic message exchange. H043's genomic adjacency is not evolutionary time, and its hypothesis is task-specific rather than a new general temporal-GNN framework. |
| Gabriel Peyré, Marco Cuturi and Justin Solomon (2016), *Gromov-Wasserstein Averaging of Kernel and Distance Matrices*, ICML/PMLR48. [Primary page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/peyre16.html) | Structural comparison without row-by-row correspondence via probabilistic maps and regularized GW optimization is established. H043's counts/normalized adjacency solve no GW/OT objective. Do not call it a new optimal-transport method. |
| Ching-Yao Chuang and Stefanie Jegelka (2022), *Tree Mover's Distance: Bridging Graph Metrics and Stability of Graph Neural Networks*. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01906) | Hierarchical OT over node attributes/GNN computation trees is established. Those computation trees are not phylogenetic genealogies; no direct equivalence to ancestral-node homology is implied. |

The limited verification set did not establish prior publication of the exact nearest-removed/nearest-added leaf assignment plus H042 flags and latent residual above. That is insufficient to claim a first. A publishable novelty argument would still need a broader search and, more decisively, evidence that correctly aligned foreign states improve the frozen task beyond the same local geometry/nonlinearity. None is currently available.
