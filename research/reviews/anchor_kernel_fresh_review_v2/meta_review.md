## Per-reviewer verdicts

- **alfa** — Major revision: the conditional construction appears sound, but novelty, comparative utility, attribution controls and task definitions need evidence.
- **bravo** — Major revision: useful changing-graph query memory is untested, and the existing probe and proposed controls leave simpler explanations open.
- **charlie** — Major revision: mathematical guarantees appear credible, but reconstructed-tree ancestry evidence, resolution validation and external validity are missing.

## Common concerns

All three reviewers distinguish a plausible representation contract from a demonstrated learned contribution. They accept the conditional PSD, bounded-message and subdivision arguments under their stated assumptions. They do not find evidence in the supplied working manuscript that the architecture improves ancestry or forecasting. Alfa's formulation is direct: “The manuscript currently contains no evidence that the proposed construction improves a learned inference task.”

All three require a precise comparison against dynamic graph kernels and state-space methods. The changing boundary generator, fixed physical impulses, reference conditioning and repeated nonlinear updates need equation-level attribution. Established covariance theory is not itself a new result. Charlie identifies the plausible distinctive scope as “the specific conjunction of persistent sample boundary reduction, exact finite-duration processing, fixed physical impulses, and the serial-subdivision/age-potential contract.”

All three ask for the census horizon, population mapping, complete feature formulas and reference-information budget. They require paired parent-level outcomes, optimizer-seed variation and independent confirmation after validation selection. None alleges demonstrated target leakage.

All three challenge the interpretation of the controls. Kernel depth changes capacity. The gain-matched diagonal arm matches a homogeneous continuous-forcing DC limit, while the actual model uses finite impulses and changing operators. Bravo gives the key distinction: “In general exp(-D t) D K^{-1} S is not exp(-K t) S.” This control retains source mixing and cannot be described as fully independent query processing.

All three ask for physical coordinate/rate units, anchor-resolution and tile-boundary sensitivity, executable configurations and numerical tolerances. Fixed-anchor exactness does not establish a continuum approximation or adequate short-tract resolution. The forecasting extension needs an explicit causal source/readout specification. Bravo and charlie additionally emphasize that fixed-topology Chickenpox cannot validate the changing-operator mechanism.

Bravo and charlie identify the separately seeded query-size cohorts as an interface-size confound. Nested queries on the same held-out parents would answer the intended scaling/generalization question more directly.

## Unique concerns

Alfa explicitly requests independent variation of age geometry in the propagator and in reference injection, with the matching ones source transformed consistently. An age benefit could otherwise arise entirely from a better instantaneous reference map.

Bravo requests controlled positive and negative targets that distinguish ordered cross-query memory from ordinary smoothing. This is a useful diagnostic before interpreting a small ancestry gain as mechanistic evidence.

## Ranking

1. **bravo**: gives the clearest finite-anchor counterexample to broad DC-equivalence language and proposes falsifiable positive/negative distinguishing tests.
2. **alfa**: identifies the generator-versus-input distinction from GraphSSM and the age-reference-injection confound, making the novelty and mechanism requirements actionable.
3. **charlie**: provides a strong deployment and resolution audit, including reconstructed trees, matched query-size cohorts and causal forecasting.

## Verdict synthesis

**Major revision.** The verdicts agree. The current draft is a coherent methods proposal with conditional structural guarantees and a labeled synthetic probe. It is not yet an evidence-backed ancestry method or an established new graph-learning contribution. The highest-priority work is the matched biological comparison with native competitors, followed by independent parent-level confirmation and the controls that distinguish graph-conditioned query memory from informative reference summaries, ordinary temporal smoothing and additional capacity. The manuscript should define the biological estimand and input budget, state the narrow inherited-versus-new contribution, and report preparation, storage, training and inference costs. New development results outside this reviewed draft do not retroactively satisfy the reviewers' assessment.

These are independent fresh-context reviews of this version. They are not conference decisions or certifications of experimental results.
