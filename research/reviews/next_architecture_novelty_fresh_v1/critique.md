H029 establishes no carrier-history benefit in this discovery condition. Static accuracy is 85.95617%, matrix 85.95587% and internal-history sham 85.95788%, against smoothing 85.93972%. They do not establish that ancestral history can never help or that the best attainable static GNN has been fitted.

Source inspection identifies several distinct limitations.

- Eight class/family fields compress donor support. H029/H030 retain complete trees and their gates can react to topology, so they are not blind to donor relationships. However, no explicit donor-dependence or effective-support statistic enters their values. A normalized vote can have the same value when one correlated lineage supports it and when several separated lineages support it. Whether the learned sweeps already recover the useful part is unresolved.
- H029 attenuates reference contrasts through repeated averaging/gating. H030 tests a better-conditioned field and a learned age potential. It is an informative normalization/kernel comparison, not a new rerooting or memory primitive. H030 was unrun when assigned and root subsequently reported it running. No H030 result was inspected here.
- The 350 cut matches the target's definition. It is not inherently a wrong readout. A point cut can nevertheless be brittle to inferred age/topology errors, and an inferred shared cut is not a certified common census ancestor.
- Native-hold reference fields contain no new within-hold variant information. The frozen genomic emitter supplies that information indirectly. H028's fixed 10kb composite route failing does not prove raw variants are uninformative. H031's forthcoming direct signal/error diagnostic can distinguish complementarity from the particular H028 readout.
- Same-age full-roster centering intentionally excludes common calibration corrections. This is a matched experimental restriction, not a universal requirement for a future architecture. Values remain reference-driven, and the residual is query-roster dependent.
- TRAIN skip predictions are explicitly in-sample. Residual fitting can therefore face a different emitter-error distribution from VAL. Its magnitude was not measured here. Two parents, 16 tiles per epoch, a zero-initialized head and 160 steps also leave information failure, optimization failure and restrictive readout failure entangled. Sites and tiles do not add independent biological replicates.

H021 already specifies census-partition exchange and clade-overlap retention. It must not be rediscovered as a new next mechanism. Earlier Kron/graph-GP designs already use covariance and Gaussian ideas. TGN, GraphSSM, Tree-LSTM and DeltaNet likewise cover the broad recurrence and tree-message ingredients. A new data route, covariance feature or useful combination alone does not establish a novel GNN.

The single provisional design below tests a missing *explicit observed support representation*. It is source-only, with no implementation or fitting recommendation before root reviews H030/H031. Its static evidence route does not yet fulfill the user's requested novel ordered graph-collection contribution. A positive matched result could supply a biological bottleneck worth building that contribution around. A negative result would make further elaboration of this particular route less compelling. Priority of the exact coupling remains unresolved.

Remaining discriminating tests are: (1) the root-owned fixed prior/uniform/constant-class conditional controls on identical spans; (2) whether observed-only confidence or support measures identify net useful SNP corrections above those controls, with supervision confined to TRAIN and predictions assessed on independent parents; (3) whether residual behavior changes under out-of-fold emitter training rather than in-sample skips, separating an error-distribution mismatch from missing signal; and (4) a p-preserving genuine-versus-independence/alignment-sham comparison for the proposed Gram.
