FindEquivalentBranches→InferBranchLengths→CombineSections→Finalize. The cached
pinned upstream C++ agrees on this dependency order. Its public_sources.json
explicitly says the installed release's build identity against that commit is
unproven; do not treat source inspection as binary qualification.

Relate.cpp's ALL runs MakeChunks unless chunk_index is supplied,then always runs
Paint/topology/equivalent-branches/branch-lengths/combine for each selected chunk.
--chunk_index is not a resume switch and skips final global Finalize. MakeChunks
rejects an already-existing temporary output directory. Individual later stages
need exact complete predecessor state. InferBranchLengths deletes painting and
hap/r/rpos/state inputs; CombineSections removes section files/parameters;
Finalize removes chunk intermediates while exporting final anc/mut. A filename,
429retained files,parameters.bin or31.07GBstat total cannot certify those stages.
No partial binary parameters/trees were opened in this investigation.

BuildTopology/InferBranchLengths source resets/advances RNG from seed,chunk and
first_section then consumes rand() through sections. Running single sections via
the parallel script cannot be assumed bitwise identical to the original all-section
ALL invocation. Do not silently change thread/section layout,seed,memory,topology,
size estimation or branch estimator to make a continuation fit.
