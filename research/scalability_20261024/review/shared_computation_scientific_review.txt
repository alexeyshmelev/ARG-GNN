TreeLoom shared computation: prior art, biological risks and matched falsification
24 October 2026. Independent source/literature review for ROOT.

Decision

Proceed with the bounded dependency census and exact layout work. Do not describe common-subexpression sharing as a new learned operator, and do not select a low-rank or approximate architecture before measuring the actual remaining bottleneck. A structural genealogy encoder with late SNP conditioning is a changed model. It merits a matched information-path experiment only if its measured compute saving is large enough to justify the biological risk.

The present 128-tree TRAIN diagnostic is an important negative result. It contains 317,897 expanded node occurrences. A topology-only census finds 2,199 unique ordered subexpressions (0.006917 unique fraction), whereas the conservative feature/source-valid first-sweep upward census finds 317,897 unique subexpressions. Structural compression therefore does not establish numerical neural-state reuse. Conversely, source identity is a sufficient key, not a necessary one: this result is a lower bound on valid reuse under that conservative policy, not proof that primitive-feature canonicalization cannot share more work. Neither census measures forward/backward speed, full-parent memory, or predictive quality.

The execution measurement also finds exact row/index agreement for an immutable layout, a 128-tree median layout rebuild of 0.025499 seconds, and cached binding of 0.000056920 seconds. These are bounded CPU measurements, not an end-to-end training speedup. The warm full-parent profile identifies learned context forward and trunk backward as much larger components than layout construction. Removing layout work alone cannot remove the learned sweep/backward cost.

Prior art and the claim boundary

1. Kelleher, Etheridge and McVean (2016), Efficient Coalescent Simulation and Genealogical Analysis for Large Sample Sizes, DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004842, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4856371/ . Sparse/coalescence-record encoding, adjacent-tree correlation and efficient sequential tree analysis predate this project. This is an antecedent for the representation and traversal opportunity, not evidence that a nonlinear ancestry GNN is already solved incrementally.

2. Kelleher et al. (2019), Inferring whole-genome histories in large population datasets, DOI 10.1038/s41588-019-0483-y, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6726478/ . The paper explicitly stores each edge shared by adjacent marginal trees once, discusses path compression, and distinguishes the tree-sequence outcome from the full history of ARG events. Thus an interval-annotated global graph is an established data representation. It does not justify applying one unconditioned node state to mutually incompatible edges from different positions.

3. Ralph, Thornton and Kelleher (2020), Efficiently Summarizing Relationships in Large Samples: A General Duality Between Statistics of Genealogies and Genomes, DOI 10.1534/genetics.120.303253, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7337078/ . The branch-statistic algorithm maintains descendant weight states under ordered edge insertions/removals, caches expensive summary-function evaluations, and propagates changed weights up ancestral paths. It is particularly close algorithmic prior art for incremental genealogy computation. Its cheap updates rely on the additive state structure. The current learned upward/outside recursions, position-dependent SNP forcing and genomic history do not inherit that complexity bound. A single changed edge can invalidate many neural states; an outside-message dependency can spread through much of the tree.

4. Whitehouse, Ray and Schrider (2024), Tree Sequences as a General-Purpose Tool for Population Genetic Inference, DOI 10.1093/molbev/msae223, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11600592/ . The inspected repository primary evidence describes complete marginal-tree graph attention, a node-sequence GRU, a tree-sequence GRU and segment geometry. Whole-tree GNN plus genomic sequence processing is established. The prospective contribution must be a qualified computational/scientific result on complete data, not the combination itself.

5. Rossi et al. (2020), Temporal Graph Networks for Deep Learning on Dynamic Graphs, https://arxiv.org/html/2006.10637v3 , Section 3. Generic graph memory, message functions and GRU/LSTM state updates are established. Event-time forecasting is not the same problem as genomic coordinate conditioning, and TGN is not asserted to be algebraically identical to this DAG. It excludes a generic novelty claim based merely on persistent state and graph changes.

6. Common-subexpression elimination, hash-consing, dynamic programming, rerooting/cavity tree recursions and autograd accumulation through shared expressions are established computational tools. A correct, efficient application can still be valuable. A defensible methods contribution would need a precisely defined dependency quotient, demonstrated favorable scaling on real frozen observed data, verified gradients, full pipeline cost, and a scientifically useful operating regime. No priority claim follows from this bounded review.

Internal antecedents matter as well. The 16 October H050 novelty/scaling report already proposed succinct edge_diffs, observation-only recipes, ancestor/outside invalidation, per-optimizer invalidation and the Omega(T*H) all-output floor. The 17 October fresh ARG operator report already specifies canonical attributed-tree intervals, physical exposure, fixed genomic output coordinates and split/merge controls, while withholding generic graph/signature novelty. H044 already tested a position-local SNP readout and lost boundary F1; H043/H045/H046 foreign-state ideas and H050 higher-order history did not meet their utility rules. None of these failures proves that the current factorization must fail, but their components cannot be reintroduced as new or assumed effective. The paired-reference-change and marker-carry reports explicitly retain uncertain utility hypotheses, not established solutions.

Exact sharing: scientific and implementation requirements

For fixed parameters, sharing is valid when every consumed primitive input and learned operator dependency is identical. Upward keys need the base computation, the exact child-message/gate dependencies, normalization degree, and original reduction order. Downward keys need parent outside/base/full aggregate, recipient edge-message subtraction and its gate. The second sweep also consumes structural/episode flags and sample-history injections. Current site pooling consumes genotype/donor/mask/panel/entropy evidence, counts/empty flags, span and global site-chunk/reduction semantics. Native node ID or equal topology alone is insufficient.

Add a primitive-input canonical census beside the conservative source-ID census. Identical immutable feature bytes, operator/parameter maps and ordered arithmetic graphs may share computation even when observations originated in different file rows. Equal current hidden vectors alone do not certify equal parameter Jacobians. Never silently quantize floats or use tolerance-based equality to manufacture exact reuse. Hashes should index candidate keys; exact key comparison must determine equality. Distinct sample/output/history routing remains intact even if some local computation is shared between identical-input samples.

Count reuse separately for base operations, gates, upward messages/states, outside states, leaf contexts, second sweep and any recurrent history. Report invalidations caused by SNP pooling, span, gates, descendants, outside context and structural/history dependencies. Diagnostic removal of a dependency may explain the ceiling, but the resulting count is a counterfactual, not valid execution of the existing model.

A shared state used k times must receive the sum of all k downstream derivatives. Preserve occurrence multiplicity, original physical/focal loss weights and full-parent recurrent order. No detached shared state, cross-update numeric cache or truncated history is permitted in an exact arm. Recompute learned values after every parameter update; immutable layouts can persist. Validate outputs, parameter gradients and one optimizer step on bounded observed packets with the same downstream multiplicities. Exercise shared descendants with different outside contexts, sampled internal nodes, roots, polytomies/unary nodes, empty-marker holds and amax ties. Synthetic indexing cases are implementation checks only. Floating-point tolerances should be registered against the FP32 baseline, including reduction-order sensitivity; bitwise equality is not required if a documented backend changes parallel scheduling.

Biological risks of the prospective factorization/refinement

A full-depth structural DAG remains biologically useful: reference relatedness, dates, branch geometry and all sampled nodes can transmit deep genealogical evidence. However, removing SNP evidence from those traversals changes which lineage interactions the model can express. A late local correction may fail to recover mutation-conditioned reference/query branch messages, allele absence patterns, recurrent/back mutations or uncertainty encoded by current support gates. Keeping every SNP in an input file is not the same as retaining its old interaction path. Low rank would introduce an additional independent bottleneck and should not be bundled with this first factorization.

Observed inferred trees and genotypes are coupled observations. Treating their learned scores as independent calibrated likelihood factors would double-count evidence without a probabilistic derivation. Reference source/age labels are permitted metadata; they are not authenticated ancestor states at a demographic census time. Stable sampled haplotypes provide correspondence. Internal inferred nodes do not establish persistent biological ancestor correspondence across arbitrary inference/serialization changes merely because IDs match.

Genealogy changes need not change source ancestry. The project's FLARE primary-source review, citing Browning, Waples and Browning (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.ajhg.2022.12.010, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9943733/ , records the pre-admixture donor-switch versus post-admixture ancestry-switch distinction. A proposed window or smooth correction can erase real short tracts while making accuracy or boundary precision look better. Deep genealogy is not nuisance by definition, and an inferred topology change is not an observed recombination/ancestry label. The fresh FLARE XML request returned HTTP500 in this pass; the biological locator here relies on the inspected earlier repository review, rather than a falsely claimed new methods retrieval.

A position-conditioned query head can vary within a native hold only if it receives coordinate-specific evidence/context. Adding a position number cannot reconstruct SNP ordering or genotype detail already discarded by pooling. Replacing holds by fixed windows mixes observations across real changes. Preserve original half-open intervals, sample IDs, marker positions, missingness, reference/query membership and focal positions. Any new output resolution is an architectural change. Artificial serialization split/merge is a useful invariance test; it is not a substitute for biological accuracy or evidence of robustness to genuine tree inference error.

Matched pilot and stopping rules

Phase A: qualify the exact execution hypothesis on frozen observed TRAIN packets. The existing first 128 trees are an indexing/dependency diagnostic, not a training cohort. Prospectively select additional deterministic packets across frozen TRAIN parents and genomic regions, preserve every input in those packets, and include all selected packets in the report. Measure source-key and exact primitive-key reuse with compilation memory/time. Do not select the most compressible block after inspecting results. If the compiled DAG plus routing/gather/autograd work is slower or larger than the expanded execution on the representative packets, stop that exact hidden-state optimization or restrict the claim to the measured useful regime. Keep the separately useful immutable-layout optimization.

Phase B: after ROOT code review/admission, compare paired cold and warm full-parent runs using the same checkpoint/parameters, full observed arrays, output/loss positions and optimizer schedule. Measure preprocessing/cache bytes, data transfer, first/second sweep forward, recomputation, backward, decoder and complete optimizer-step wall time, peak host PSS/RSS, peak GPU allocated/reserved bytes and complete-parent updates per hour. Include original observation/inference cost and cache build/write/read cost separately and amortized at the actual pass count. A profile or GPU activity sample alone is not a scalability result. Do not extrapolate the 128-tree layout microbenchmark to the full parent without measuring it.

Phase C is conditional on a definite new-model specification. Freeze that specification and its biological hypothesis before quality outcomes. If the candidate changes both SNP interaction placement and output refinement, use a matched 2-by-2 comparison: original early SNP interaction versus structural/late interaction, each with the original readout versus the same new positional readout. Keep structural width, parameter accounting and optimization budgets explicit; equal parameter count alone does not establish equal capacity. This separates a speed/interaction tradeoff from a resolution effect. Do not claim the larger combined change is caused by either component without these controls. Keep the frozen TreeLoom/native methods as comparison arms; historical scores alone are not matched controls.

Use the existing complete frozen TRAIN parents and unchanged observed trees/genotypes/reference-query identities. Never alter simulation parameters, labels, splits or input inference results to create favorable speed or accuracy. Exact diagnostic slices do not license subset training as a full-data result. Fit all arms on TRAIN, select on validation with one frozen criterion, and evaluate TEST only after architecture/hyperparameters are sealed. Reused validation parents remain exploratory. Additional seeds, when admitted, should be paired across arms. Report both equal full-parent update/data budgets and equal wall-time budgets; neither alone identifies the whole tradeoff.

For American data, the existing sparse 10,000 focals can support focal ancestry accuracy, per-class recall/F1 and NLL/calibration at that support. They cannot establish dense tract-boundary quality. Use already qualified dense truth only in evaluation where the existing dataset supplies it; no new dense training truth or altered simulated dataset is proposed. If no matched dense evaluation exists, explicitly defer the boundary claim. For the European dense evaluator, report typed boundary precision/recall/F1, missed/extra boundaries, false short islands, false-switched bp/Mb, true short-tract recall and accuracy versus distance to a true transition. Bootstrap/uncertainty must group by independent parent, not treat correlated sites or haplotypes as independent biological replicates.

Register numerical noninferiority/efficiency criteria before comparative outcomes. A reasonable exploratory quality proposal, requiring ROOT acceptance rather than treated as an existing registration, is at most 0.05 percentage point equal-parent span/focal accuracy loss, no more than 1.00 point per-class recall loss, no mean NLL regression, and no dense 1 kb boundary-F1 or recall of true tracts no longer than 10 kb loss where those metrics are qualified. Pair this with a concrete end-to-end gate such as at least 20 percent lower median warm full-parent optimizer-step time or peak memory sufficient for the unchanged 30 Mb validation parent. Report confidence intervals and every metric; a point estimate passing a margin is not strong noninferiority evidence with five parents. If precision gains come from deleting real short tracts, reject the biological mechanism. If late conditioning is no faster after compilation/routing, reject the scalability rationale. If only positional refinement helps, attribute the quality gain to the readout and withhold the DAG/factorization explanation.

What this review establishes

Exact layout reuse has measured bounded evidence. Exact feature-valid hidden-state reuse is not yet shown profitable. A changed structural/position-local model has a plausible but risky information-path hypothesis, not established novelty or superiority. The most valuable next scientific result may be a negative reuse census and a cleanly measured exact execution improvement. A stronger method claim requires a new, explicit biological/computational tradeoff that survives matched controls on unchanged data.

Scope: source, small metadata/measurement reports, prior review text and public primary literature only. This reviewer did not read truth/checkpoint/scientific array payloads, execute a model or GPU task, change simulations/training inputs, touch shared running source, signal processes, submit work or perform Git operations. Apply-script preparation/checking was a separate authorized operational deliverable; ROOT alone invoked its mutation path.
