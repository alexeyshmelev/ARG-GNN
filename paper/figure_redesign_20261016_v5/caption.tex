Ancestors carry age $\tau$ and sampled/root indicators. \textbf{(c)} $N_t$ counts nodes, $S$ sampled haplotypes and $d$ feature width. Two complete upward/downward GNN traversals share weights. All $S$ sampled embeddings remain aligned. $e_{tq}$ embeds haplotype $q$ in tree $t$. Magenta follows $q_1$. \textbf{(d)} One shared, one-layer BiGRU processes each sampled sequence with log-scaled interval span $w_t$ and neighboring-center distances $\Delta c_t^{\pm}$. $\Vert$ concatenates local and recurrent embeddings.
