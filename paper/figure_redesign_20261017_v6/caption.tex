\caption{\textbf{(a,b)} Three intervals of an illustrative simulated ARG yield matching local trees. All haplotypes supply alleles. References also supply ancestry vectors. Query ancestry is unknown. Ancestors carry age $\tau$ and sampled/root indicators. \textbf{(c)} $N_t$ counts nodes in tree $t$, $S$ sampled haplotypes and $d$ feature width. Two GNN passes each traverse every node upward then downward with shared weights. Generic forks summarize proper-clade presence across the trees. Gained/lost and consecutive-clade updates use each node's own state. Tiny boxes show the actual 350-generation cut groups. The same five history rows persist, with $q_1$ in magenta. Open vectors show its current group context $f$. Filled rows show sample histories $z$, scanned independently in both directions with retention $\rho_t=\exp(-w_t/\lambda)$ and current weight $1-\rho_t$. Here $\lambda$ is a learned physical length scale. $\bar z$ averages both directions and the current group's sample rows. Subtracting $f$ and linearly projecting supplies a residual before pass two. Readout retains $S$ separate per-sample vectors $e_{tq}$ for each tree. \textbf{(d)} One shared, one-layer BiGRU reads each sampled sequence with physical span $w_t$ and neighboring-center distances $\Delta c_t^\pm$, transformed by $\log(1+\cdot)$. $\Vert$ concatenates local and recurrent embeddings. The MLP and softmax give ancestry probabilities $p_{tq}$, and argmax gives interval calls. Values and predictions are schematic.}
